## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your light foot soldiers, the archers and gunners ([archers](../units/archer.md), [celestial archers](../units/celestial-archer.md), [Pagan archers](../units/pagan-archer.md), [gunners](../units/gunner.md)) better armour. This is the first of three steps; it is offered from the start.

## Researched at

- **Researched at:** [Blacksmith](../buildings/blacksmith.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing beyond the building and the cost.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->

<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Light Infantry Leather Armour`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
