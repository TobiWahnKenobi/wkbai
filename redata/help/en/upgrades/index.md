# Upgrades and research

Every research you can buy, grouped by what it improves. A research is a button on the building that offers it; it costs resources and takes time. Once started it runs to the end, and units ordered at the same building wait until it is done. Most research works at once for every unit of the kind it improves, the ones already in the field included.

![A Blacksmith with its research buttons, one research under way](shot:gamedata-research)

The weapon and armour lines at the [Blacksmith](../buildings/blacksmith.md) come in three steps: the second waits for your Manor to become a Keep, the third for the Castle.
