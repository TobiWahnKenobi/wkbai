## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your light cavalry ([javelineers](../units/javelineer.md), [mounted bowwomen](../units/pagan-mounted-bowoman.md), [mounted hand gunners](../units/mounted-hand-gunner.md)) better weapons. This is the last of three steps; it is offered once your Manor has grown into a Castle, and needs the second step.

## Researched at

## Needs

- building a [University](../buildings/university.md)
- Shown from the third Manor level (Castle) on.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

<!-- gen:commands -->

<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
