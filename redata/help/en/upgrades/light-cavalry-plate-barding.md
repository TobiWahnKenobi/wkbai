## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your light cavalry ([javelineers](../units/javelineer.md), [mounted bowwomen](../units/pagan-mounted-bowoman.md), [mounted hand gunners](../units/mounted-hand-gunner.md)) better armour. This is the last of three steps; it is offered once your Manor has grown into a Castle, and needs the second step.

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
