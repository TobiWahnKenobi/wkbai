## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your heavy foot soldiers ([spearmen](../units/spearman.md), [pikemen](../units/pikeman.md), [men at arms](../units/man-at-arms.md), the Pagan spearwomen and pikemen, barbarians, mercenaries and militia) better armour. This is the second of three steps; it is offered once your Manor has grown into a Keep, and needs the first step.

## Researched at

- **Researched at:** [Blacksmith](../buildings/blacksmith.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Monastery](../buildings/monastery.md) or building a [Sawmill](../buildings/sawmill.md) or building a [Sacred Grove](../buildings/temple.md) or building a [Windmill](../buildings/windmill.md)
- Shown from the second Manor level (Keep) on.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
