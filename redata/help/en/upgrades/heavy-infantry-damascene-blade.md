## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your heavy foot soldiers ([spearmen](../units/spearman.md), [pikemen](../units/pikeman.md), [men at arms](../units/man-at-arms.md), the Pagan spearwomen and pikemen, barbarians, mercenaries and militia) better weapons. This is the first of three steps; it is offered from the start.

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
