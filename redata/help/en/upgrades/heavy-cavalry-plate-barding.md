## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your heavy cavalry ([mounted spearmen](../units/mounted-spearman.md), [knights](../units/knight.md), [heavy horsemen](../units/heavy-horseman.md), [monastic knights](../units/monastic-knight.md)) better armour. This is the last of three steps; it is offered once your Manor has grown into a Castle, and needs the second step.

## Researched at

<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [University](../buildings/university.md)
- Shown from the third Manor level (Castle) on.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- No new buttons: it improves what you already have.
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Heavy Cavalry Plate Barding`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
