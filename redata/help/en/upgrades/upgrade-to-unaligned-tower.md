# Upgrade Wall to Fortified Tower

## Role

Raises one section of [stone wall](../buildings/unaligned-wall.md) into a [stone tower](../buildings/unaligned-tower-stone.md). The wall section itself offers it.

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Fortified Wall](../buildings/unaligned-wall.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- **Turns units or buildings into, or calls up:** [Fortified Tower](../buildings/unaligned-tower-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Upgrade to Unaligned Tower`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Towers at corners and next to gates cover the most wall.
