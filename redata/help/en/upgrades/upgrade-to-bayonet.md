## Role

A research at the University or Scriptorium that gives your gunners a plug bayonet, so they can fight hand to hand as well as shoot. Existing gunners get it too.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Scriptorium](../buildings/scriptorium.md), [University](../buildings/university.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- [Gunpowder](gunpowder.md) or [Celestial Gunpowder](celestial-gunpowder.md) or building a [University](../buildings/university.md)
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- **Unlocks:** [Gunner with Bayonet](../units/gunner-bayonet.md)
- **Turns units or buildings into, or calls up:** [Gunner with Bayonet](../units/gunner-bayonet.md)
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Upgrade to Bayonet`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- See the [Gunner with Bayonet](../units/gunner-bayonet.md) page for switching between the two.
