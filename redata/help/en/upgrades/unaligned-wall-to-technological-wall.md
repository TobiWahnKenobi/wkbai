# Unaligned Wall to Technological Wall

<!-- gen:facts -->
<!-- facts:static -->
| | |
|---|---|
| Research time | 180 s |
<!-- /facts:static -->
<!-- /gen:facts -->

## Role

The second wall research at the Guildhall: your stone walls, towers and gates are rebuilt as stronger [Renaissance walls](../buildings/technological-wall.md). It needs a Monastery or an Observatory.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Guildhall](../buildings/guildhall-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Monastery](../buildings/monastery.md) or building a [Observatory](../buildings/observatory.md)
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- **Unlocks:** [Technological Gatehouse](../buildings/technological-gatehouse-closed.md), [Technological Wall](../buildings/technological-wall.md), [Unaligned Wall](../buildings/unaligned-wall.md), [Upgrade to Technological Tower](upgrade-to-technological-tower.md)
- **Turns units or buildings into, or calls up:** [Technological Wall](../buildings/technological-wall.md), [Technological Tower](../buildings/technological-tower-stone.md), [Technological Gatehouse](../buildings/technological-gatehouse-closed.md)
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Unaligned Wall to Technological Wall`.
