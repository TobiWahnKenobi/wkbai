# Stockade to Unaligned Wall

<!-- gen:facts -->
<!-- facts:static -->
| | |
|---|---|
| Cost | 500 wood |
| Research time | 120 s |
<!-- /facts:static -->
<!-- /gen:facts -->

## Role

The first wall research at the Guildhall: your wooden stockades become stone walls, and peasants build [stone walls](../buildings/unaligned-wall.md) and [gatehouses](../buildings/unaligned-gatehouse-closed.md) from then on.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Guildhall](../buildings/guildhall-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Guildhall](../buildings/guildhall-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- **Unlocks:** [Stockade](../buildings/stockade.md), [Unaligned Gatehouse](../buildings/unaligned-gatehouse-closed.md), [Unaligned Wall](../buildings/unaligned-wall.md), [Upgrade to Unaligned Tower](upgrade-to-unaligned-tower.md)
- **Turns units or buildings into, or calls up:** [Unaligned Wall](../buildings/unaligned-wall.md), [Unaligned Gatehouse](../buildings/unaligned-gatehouse-closed.md)
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Stockade to Unaligned Wall`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Stone walls are five times as strong as a stockade.
