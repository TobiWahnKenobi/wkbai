## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your heavy cavalry ([mounted spearmen](../units/mounted-spearman.md), [knights](../units/knight.md), [heavy horsemen](../units/heavy-horseman.md), [monastic knights](../units/monastic-knight.md)) better armour. This is the first of three steps; it is offered from the start.

<!-- /gen:trained -->

<!-- /gen:needs -->

<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Heavy Cavalry Leather Barding`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
