## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your light foot soldiers, the archers and gunners ([archers](../units/archer.md), [celestial archers](../units/celestial-archer.md), [Pagan archers](../units/pagan-archer.md), [gunners](../units/gunner.md)) better weapons. This is the last of three steps; it is offered once your Manor has grown into a Castle, and needs the second step.

## Needs

- Shown from the third Manor level (Castle) on.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

<!-- gen:leads -->

<!-- gen:commands -->

<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
