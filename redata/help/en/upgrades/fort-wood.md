# Wooden Forts

<!-- gen:facts -->
<!-- facts:static -->
| | |
|---|---|
| Cost | 300 wood |
| Research time | 180 s |
<!-- /facts:static -->
<!-- /gen:facts -->

## Role

The Guildhall's fort plans: after this research, peasants can build the [wooden fort](../buildings/fort-wood.md) (a Barracks is needed too), and watch towers can be turned into forts.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Guildhall](../buildings/guildhall-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Monastery](../buildings/monastery.md) or building a [Sawmill](../buildings/sawmill.md) or building a [Temple](../buildings/temple.md) or building a [Windmill](../buildings/windmill.md)
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- **Unlocks:** [Fort (stone)](../buildings/fort-stone.md), [Fort (wooden)](../buildings/fort-wood.md), [Upgrade to Wooden Fort](upgrade-to-wooden-fort.md)
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Fort Wood`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Forts guard outlying Villages well.
