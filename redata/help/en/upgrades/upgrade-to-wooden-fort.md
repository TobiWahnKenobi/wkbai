## Role

Turns a [Watch Tower](../buildings/watch-tower.md) into a [wooden fort](../buildings/fort-wood.md). Researched at the tower itself; it needs the [Fort Upgrade](fort-wood.md) research.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Watch Tower](../buildings/watch-tower.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

- [Fort Upgrade](fort-wood.md)
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- **Turns units or buildings into, or calls up:** [Fort](../buildings/fort-wood.md)
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Upgrade to Wooden Fort`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Cheaper than building a new fort where a tower already stands.
