## Role

A Guildhall research that again raises how many people each farmer feeds. It needs a Windmill or a Sawmill.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Guildhall](../buildings/guildhall-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Sawmill](../buildings/sawmill.md) or building a [Windmill](../buildings/windmill.md)
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- No new buttons: it improves what you already have.
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Horse Harness`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Research it after the [Heavy Plough](heavy-plough.md) when your army outgrows your farms.
