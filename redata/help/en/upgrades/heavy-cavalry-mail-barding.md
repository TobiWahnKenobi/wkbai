## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your heavy cavalry ([mounted spearmen](../units/mounted-spearman.md), [knights](../units/knight.md), [heavy horsemen](../units/heavy-horseman.md), [monastic knights](../units/monastic-knight.md)) better armour. This is the second of three steps; it is offered once your Manor has grown into a Keep, and needs the first step.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Blacksmith](../buildings/blacksmith.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Monastery](../buildings/monastery.md) or building a [Sawmill](../buildings/sawmill.md) or building a [Temple](../buildings/temple.md) or building a [Windmill](../buildings/windmill.md)
- Shown from the second Manor level (Keep) on.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

## Commands

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
