## Role

A Blacksmith research that gives your light cavalry ([javelineers](../units/javelineer.md), [mounted bowwomen](../units/pagan-mounted-bowoman.md), [mounted hand gunners](../units/mounted-hand-gunner.md)) better weapons. This is the first of three steps; it is offered from the start.

## Researched at

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Researched at:** [Blacksmith](../buildings/blacksmith.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing beyond the building and the cost.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- No new buttons: it improves what you already have.
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- The research is its own button: `Research Light Cavalry Iron-tipped Weapon`.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Each step costs more than the one before: research the line your army is built on.
- The research works for units already in the field as well as new ones.
