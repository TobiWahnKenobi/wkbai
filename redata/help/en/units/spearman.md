## Role

The spearman is the cheapest regular soldier: 60 food at the [Barracks](../buildings/barracks-stone.md), no research needed. It has 120 hit points and a solid front (armour 8 from the front, 1 from behind).

## Tips

- Spearmen are good early defenders and good against horsemen that charge straight into their front.
- Their rear armour is almost nothing: keep the line facing the enemy and protect the flanks.
