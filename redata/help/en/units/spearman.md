## Role

The spearman is the cheapest regular soldier: 60 food at the [Barracks](../buildings/barracks-stone.md), no research needed. It has 120 hit points, a 30-point blow every 2.5 seconds and armour 8 from the front, 4 on the sides and 1 from behind. It is heavy infantry: its job is to hold a line and to stop horsemen.

## Strong against

- **Heavy cavalry from the front.** A [Knight](knight.md) hits a spearman's front for 21 (29 − 8); the spearman hits back for 18 (30 − 12). A [Battle Rider](heavy-horseman.md) takes 20 a blow.
- **Cavalry charging a formed line.** Heavy cavalry that strikes the front of formed heavy infantry rears up after its blow and rides 25 to 35 m away, unless it attacks in a Wedge. A spear line in formation therefore keeps breaking cavalry charges.
- **Siege engines that reach it.** A [Mangonel](mangonel.md) has armour 5 at the front: 25 a blow.
- The game itself rates horsemen and rams as the best targets for heavy infantry: the [attack cursor](../mechanics/combat.md#unit-classes) turns green over them.

## Weak against

- **Missile troops.** Light cavalry rates spearmen as its ideal target. A [Mounted Huntress](pagan-mounted-bowoman.md) or [Dragoon](mounted-hand-gunner.md) is three times as fast (speed 12 against 3.6) and its 18 to 20-point shots still do 10 to 12 through the front, 17 to 19 in the back.
- **Heavier foot.** A [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) hits it for 27 and takes only 18 back.
- **Flanks and rear.** Armour 4 on the sides and 1 behind: a knight from behind does 28 a blow.
- **Splash.** Stones from mangonels and trebuchets hurt everyone within 8 m, so packed spearmen lose men to every shot.

## Tips

- Put spearmen in a [Line](../commands/formation.md) facing the enemy horse: the Line raises attack and armour by a fifth ([formations](../mechanics/formations.md#bonuses)), and charging cavalry bounces off its front.
- Against archers, form an Orb: heavy infantry in an Orb take 30% less from missiles.
- Use them early; later in the game a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) or [Warrioress of the Dawn](pagan-spearwoman.md) does the same job better.
- To beat spearmen, shoot them with mounted archers or hit them from the side.
