## Role

The Fomorian Beast is a Pagan giant that throws rocks, from the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md) for 400 food. It needs the [Summon the Fomorians](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-the-formorians.md) or a [University](../buildings/university.md). It has 500 hit points, armour 15 at the front (11.75 on the sides, 1.5 behind) and throws 35-point rocks (50 to buildings) about every 3 seconds, from 15 m out to 95 m. It is a demon, and can't enter forest or rocky ground.

## Abilities

- **[Throw Rock](../commands/throw-rock.md):** out of range, it walks closer by itself. Every stone also hurts all units within 8 m of where it lands, yours and your allies' included, by a tenth of its damage less each one's armour.
- **Ammunition:** a full charge bar holds three rocks; the bar refills by about 1 a second, and much faster near a [Supply Wagon](supply-wagon.md), so after a quick burst of three it throws about one rock every 28 seconds.
- It has no melee attack at all.
- Arrows do it only a quarter of their damage.

## Strong against

- **Buildings and other siege engines**, as for every siege weapon.
- **Packed infantry**: 27 a rock through a [Spearman](spearman.md)'s front, with splash.
- **Archers**, whose arrows barely hurt it.

## Weak against

- **Exorcism**: as a demon it takes 100 from a [Priest](priest.md)'s exorcism.
- **Cavalry from behind** (armour 1.5): a [Knight](knight.md) does 28 a blow, and the beast can't strike back.
- **Gunners**: bullets do 5 a shot from the front, 18 from behind.

## Tips

- Its first three rocks come fast: open on the most important target.
- Keep a [Supply Wagon](supply-wagon.md) near it to keep the rocks coming.
- It can't defend itself: always leave troops with it.
