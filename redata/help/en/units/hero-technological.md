## Role

King Artos is the campaign's Renaissance hero: 400 hit points, armour 24 at the front, 14 at the sides and 4 at the rear, a 55-point blow every second and a sight of 17. Unlike the other forms of Artos he can also repair buildings and engines. He is not trained; the missions bring him in.

## Abilities

- **[Repair](../commands/repair.md):** repairs your buildings as fast as a peasant does, at the same cost.
- **[Repair (Character)](../commands/repair-character.md):** repairs siege engines, ships and carts from within 5 m: 3 hit points per swing of his hammer, for 1 material each.
- **Heals himself and others.** When he is idle, hurt and no enemy is within 30 m, he uses 10 points of his charge bar (60 at most) each second to heal 15 hit points (25 after [Surgery](../upgrades/surgery.md)), then heals other hurt units within 30 m.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Cavalry and missile troops.** 55 a second: 43 against a knight's front, 53 against an archer. Knights do him 5 from the front, arrows 1.

## Weak against

- **Heavy infantry**: a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) does 11 a blow, and three at a time add up. The game rates heavy infantry as good against him.
- **Blows from behind**: his rear armour is 4.

## Tips

- Use his repair on damaged engines and walls between fights.
- He is the only Artos who does not make enemy spells fizzle: keep a cleric near him against Pagan magic.
