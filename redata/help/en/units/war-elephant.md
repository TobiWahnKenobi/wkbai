## Role

The Imperial War Elephant is a living siege engine of the pure Imperial path: 500 hit points, armour 22 all round and 50-point blows every 4 seconds that do 100 damage to buildings. Archers ride on its back. It costs 1000 food and 50 gold at the [Armoury](../buildings/armoury-stone.md), eats 5 food, and needs a [Statue of the Archangel](../buildings/michael-statue.md).

## Abilities

- **Trampling blows:** each blow also hits up to 4 more enemy units within 5 m of its target with the same damage. Its reach is long (about 11 m against a spearman).
- **Archers on its back:** every 5 seconds they loose 6 arrows (15 damage each) at up to three of the nearest enemies within 45 m. You can't tell them to hold fire.
- Enemy archers and horse archers set to skirmish back away from it.
- Arrows do it only a quarter of their damage.

## Strong against

- **Massed infantry**: 42 a blow to a [Spearman](spearman.md) and to the men around him.
- **Cavalry**: 38 a blow to a [Knight](knight.md), which does only 7 back.
- **Archers and gunners**: arrows do nothing, bullets 1.
- **Walls and gates**: 100 structure a blow.

## Weak against

- **Heavy siege and rockets**: a [Trebuchet](trebuchet.md) stone does 53, rockets ignore armour.
- **Giants**: a [Stone Elemental](stone-elemental-large.md) does 78 a blow.
- **The heaviest pikes**: a [Warrior of the Sun](pagan-pikeman.md) does 18 a blow; a crowd of them wears it down.
- **Your food**: it costs 1000 food and 5 upkeep.

## Tips

- Send it straight at walls and gates; it shrugs off the defenders' arrows.
- Drive it into the thickest enemy block: every blow hits up to five men.
- Escort it against enemy siege engines and rocket launchers.
