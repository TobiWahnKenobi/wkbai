## Role

The succubus is a Pagan temptress from the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md). She can charm enemy units to your side, possess peasants, drive troops into a battle frenzy and defile holy buildings.

## Trained at / Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Trained at:** [Henge](../buildings/henge.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

## Tips

- [Charm](../commands/charm.md) steals the enemy's best units one at a time.
- [Berserker Rage](../commands/make-baresark.md) turns your own troops into frenzied fighters for a while.
- Enemy inquisitors can banish her: keep her away from them.
