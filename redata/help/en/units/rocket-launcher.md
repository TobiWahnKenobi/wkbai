## Role

The rocket launcher fires volleys of rockets. A [Sapper](sapper.md) builds it for 500 wood and 500 gold after [Rocketry](../upgrades/rocketry.md). It has 400 hit points and must be unlimbered to fire.

## Tips

- [Limber](../commands/limber.md) it to move and [Unlimber](../commands/unlimber.md) it to shoot.
- Rockets are best against packed formations.
