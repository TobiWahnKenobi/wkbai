## Role

The barbarian is a cheap hired fighter from the [Trading Post](../buildings/trading-post.md): 50 food, a hard-hitting 27-point attack every 1.5 seconds, but only 50 hit points. It needs a [Maypole](../buildings/maypole.md) and is not offered on the Imperial path.

## Tips

- Barbarians are glass cannons: use them in numbers, quickly, before archers wear them down.
- They can set fire to buildings with [Arson Attack](../commands/arson.md).
