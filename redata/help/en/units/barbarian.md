## Role

The barbarian is a cheap hired raider from the [Trading Post](../buildings/trading-post.md): 50 food for a 27-point blow every 1.5 seconds, but only 50 hit points and armour 5 at the front, 0 at the rear. It needs a [Maypole](../buildings/maypole.md), is not offered with Imperial buildings and cannot form up. Hostile barbarians can also come out of barbarian huts on the map when barbarians are switched on.

## Abilities

- **[Arson Attack](../commands/arson.md):** the barbarian stands at an enemy building for 15 s, then sets it on fire; fire proofing does not help. An attack order on a building becomes arson by itself.
- **[Take Cart](../commands/take-cart.md):** it can take over a cart whose driver was killed.
- It runs away from [Abaddon](abaddon.md) and the [Behemoth](behemoth.md) when idle.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Damage for its price.** 27 every 1.5 s for 50 food: 25 against an archer, 15 against a knight's front and 26 against its rear.
- **Siege engines and buildings**: the game rates it very well against siege engines, and arson burns down undefended towns.

## Weak against

- **Missiles.** An arrow does it 10, a bullet 15: a few volleys kill it before it arrives.
- **Heavy cavalry and heavy infantry.** A knight does 24 a blow, a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) 30: two blows each.
- **Light cavalry**: the game rates javelin riders and mounted archers well against it.

## Tips

- Use barbarians in numbers, quickly, against enemy engines, archers' flanks and outlying buildings.
- Send a few to burn farms and villages while your army keeps the enemy busy.
- Against barbarians, shoot them on the way in; they have no armour to speak of.
