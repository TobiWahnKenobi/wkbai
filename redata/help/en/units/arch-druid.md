## Role

The arch druid is the senior Pagan magic user from the [Temple of the Moon](../buildings/black-mass.md). He wakes elementals out of the land (wood, stone and gold), entangles enemies, hardens your troops' skin and regrows forests.

## Tips

- [Awaken Wood Elemental](../commands/awaken-wood-elemental.md), stone and gold elementals turn a forest, a rock or a gold seam into a fighting giant.
- [Greengrow](../commands/greengrow.md) replants trees for your woodcutters.
- He costs 250 food and 150 gold and has 60 hit points: protect him.
