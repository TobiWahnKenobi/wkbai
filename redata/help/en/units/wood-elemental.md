## Role

The Wood Elemental is a tree woken by an [Arch Druid](arch-druid.md): 200 hit points, a 40-point blow every 2 seconds with a long reach, armour 15 on every side and speed 5. It is a demon: it eats nothing and can't be healed.

## Abilities

- **[Awaken Wood Elemental](../commands/awaken-wood-elemental.md):** the druid stands next to a tree and spends 75 charge. The tree shrinks for 15 seconds and the elemental steps out of it. No building is needed.
- **Lasts** until it is killed or its druid dies; then it turns back into a tree.
- While it lives, its druid's charge refills a quarter slower; that comes back when the elemental dies.
- It takes only a quarter of an arrow's damage.

## Strong against

- **Archers and light foot**: 38 a blow to an [Archer](archer.md), and arrows do nothing through its armour.
- **Spearmen**: 32 a blow through a [Spearman](spearman.md)'s front, which does 15 back.

## Weak against

- **Exorcism**: a [Priest](priest.md) does 100 to it, half its hit points.
- **Heavy infantry**: a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) does 20 a blow, a [Warrior of the Sun](pagan-pikeman.md) 25.
- **Gunners**: bullets do 5 a shot, arrows nothing.

## Tips

- It is cheap: wake one in any wood next to a fight, and wake more while the druid has charge.
- Use its speed (5) and reach to catch archers.
- Keep it away from enemy priests.
