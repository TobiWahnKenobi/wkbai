## Role

The Galleass is the heaviest warship: 700 hit points, armour 22 on every side and a cannon that fires 50-point balls (35 to buildings) every 10 seconds out to about 150 m. It costs 800 wood and 100 gold. It never runs out of shot.

## Abilities

- **[Fire (Bombard)](../commands/fire-bombard.md):** 50 damage and 35 structure damage, every 10 s. Every unit within 8 m of the hit, yours included, takes a tenth of the damage. Unlike the land bombard it has no shortest range and no ammunition limit.
- **[Repair (Docking)](../commands/docking.md):** within 25 m of your dock it is repaired by 10 hit points a second, for 1 wood a second.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Other ships.** 42 a ball to a [Corsair](arrow-boat.md), 25 to a [Cog](cog.md)'s bow and 40 to its side; a [Fire Boat](fire-boat.md) dies to two balls.
- **Coastal towns and troops on the shore**, from far out of their reach.

## Weak against

- **Fire Boats in numbers.** Each does 8 an arrow through its armour, three arrows every 2 s, and they are fast.
- **Slow shots.** One ball every 10 s misses moving targets easily.

## Tips

- It rules the water and shells coastal towns from out of their reach.
- Keep Corsairs with it to sink Fire Boats before they close in.
