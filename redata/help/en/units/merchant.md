## Role

The merchant trades: it turns goods into gold at a shop or market and carries the money back. Trained at the [Warehouse](../buildings/warehouse-stone.md), it costs 100 food and 20 gold and has no real fighting value.

## Tips

- Merchants are a steady source of gold when your mines run dry.
- Keep their route short and away from enemy patrols.
