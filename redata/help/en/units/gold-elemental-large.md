## Role

The gold elemental is a giant woken by an [Arch Druid](arch-druid.md) from a gold deposit. Its size depends on the gold that was left: from a full deposit of 200 it has 500 hit points, 100-point blows and 30 damage to buildings; a smaller deposit gives a smaller, weaker but slightly faster elemental. Armour 30 on every side; speed 2.

## Abilities

- **Arrow armour.** Arrows and javelins do only a quarter of their damage.
- **Shrinks with damage.** As it loses hit points it shrinks, and its blows and its reach shrink with it, until it is down to 200 hit points.
- **Turns back to gold.** When the druid dies, it falls apart into small gold piles of 10 each, one for every 100 hit points it had left. Killed outright, it leaves nothing.
- It slows the druid's charge bar by half while it lives.
- **Demonic:** holy fire does it double damage and [Exorcise Demons](../commands/exorcism.md) works on it.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Almost everything that hits it.** Armour 30 all round: arrows, knights and spearmen do it nothing, a gunner's bullet 1.
- **Light cavalry**: the game rates it well against riders who come close.
- **Formations**: 100 a blow kills most soldiers outright.

## Weak against

- **Heavy hitters.** A [Trebuchet](trebuchet.md) does 45, a [Bombard](bombard.md) 15, a [Warrior of the Sun](pagan-pikeman.md) 10 a blow.
- **[Inquisitors](inquisitor.md)**: Exorcise Demons up to 200, holy flame 30.
- **Speed.** At speed 2 it cannot catch anything; troops simply walk away from it.
- **Its druid.** Kill the [Arch Druid](arch-druid.md) and it collapses.

## Tips

- Wake it from a full deposit; a small deposit gives a small elemental.
- Wake it where the fight will come to it, for example in front of your gate.
- Against it, go for the druid.
