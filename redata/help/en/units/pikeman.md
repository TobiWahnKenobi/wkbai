## Role

The Man-at-Arms is the heavier foot soldier of the Imperial side: 150 hit points, a 35-point blow every 2.5 seconds and armour 12 from the front, 6 on the sides and 1 from behind. It costs 60 food and 30 wood and needs [Chivalry](../upgrades/chivalry.md) (or a [Statue of the Archangel](../buildings/michael-statue.md)) before the [Barracks](../buildings/barracks-stone.md) can train it. It is heavy infantry, the anvil of an Imperial army.

## Strong against

- **Heavy cavalry from the front.** It hits a [Knight](knight.md) for 23 a blow and takes 17. Against a [Squire](mounted-spearman.md) it is 27 against 13.
- **Cavalry charging a formed line.** Heavy cavalry that strikes the front of formed heavy infantry rears up and rides away after one blow, unless it attacks in a Wedge.
- **Lighter foot.** It hits a [Spearman](spearman.md) for 27 and takes 18; a [Militia](militia.md) man lasts three blows.
- **Arrows from the front.** An [Archer](archer.md)'s 15-point arrow does only 3 through armour 12.

## Weak against

- **Mounted archers and gunners**, which the game rates as its worst enemy: they outrun it (speed 12 against 3) and shoot it in the flank (armour 6) and back (armour 1).
- **Flanks and rear.** A knight from behind does 28 a blow.
- **Siege stones**: a [Mangonel](mangonel.md) does 18 through the front, and the splash hurts everyone within 8 m.
- **Bigger heavy infantry**: a [Warrior of the Sun](pagan-pikeman.md) hits it for 28 and takes 17.

## Tips

- Keep Men-at-Arms in a [Line](../commands/formation.md) in front of your archers; it raises attack and armour by a fifth.
- Turn the line towards the threat: the front is armoured 12 times better than the back.
- They are slow (speed 3): let cavalry do the chasing.
- Building a [Statue of the Archangel](../buildings/michael-statue.md) turns your Men-at-Arms into [Imperial Pikemen](man-at-arms.md).
