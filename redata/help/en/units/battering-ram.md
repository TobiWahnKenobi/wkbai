## Role

The battering ram breaks gates and walls. A [Sapper](sapper.md) builds it on the spot for 200 wood; it needs no crew. It has 150 hit points, armour 15 on every side and arrow armour, and does 40 damage to buildings with every blow, every 2 seconds, from up to 10 m away. It is slow (speed 3.5) and cannot climb slopes steeper than 30°.

## Abilities

- **Battering:** 40 structure damage a blow. Against soldiers it does only 15.
- **Arrow armour:** arrows and javelins do only a quarter of their damage, which against armour 15 is nothing.
- It can be carried in a [Cog](cog.md); it takes the room of 5 soldiers.

## Strong against

- **Gates, walls and buildings.** That is all it is for.
- **Archers and javelins**: they cannot hurt it at all.

## Weak against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Any soldier who reaches it.** A knight does 14 a blow, a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) 20, an [Imperial Pikeman](man-at-arms.md) 23, and it has only 150 hit points. Every soldier is rated as good against it.
- **Gunners** (5 a bullet) and siege engines.

## Tips

- Escort it: it cannot defend itself against soldiers.
- Shoot the archers off the wall first; they cannot hurt the ram, but they will hurt its escort.
- Against a ram, send any melee troops out of the gate: even militia wear it down.
