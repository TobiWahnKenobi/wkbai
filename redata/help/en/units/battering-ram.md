## Role

The battering ram breaks gates and walls. A [Sapper](sapper.md) builds it on the spot for 200 wood; it then rolls out with 150 hit points and armour 15 all round and does 40 damage to buildings per blow.

## Trained at / Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Built by:** [Sapper](sapper.md) (the engine is put up on the spot like a building, then rolls out)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Tips

- Escort it: it cannot defend itself against soldiers.
- Aim it at gatehouses, which are usually weaker than the wall around them.
