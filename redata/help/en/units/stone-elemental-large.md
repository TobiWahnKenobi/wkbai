## Role

The Stone Elemental is a giant of rock woken by an [Arch Druid](arch-druid.md) from a rock outcrop, once you have built an [Arcanum](../buildings/arcanum.md). At full size it has 500 hit points, 100-point blows and armour 30 on every side, but crawls along at speed 2. It is a demon: it eats nothing and can't be healed.

## Abilities

- **[Awaken Stone Elemental](../commands/awaken-stone-elemental.md):** the druid stands next to a rock outcrop and spends charge equal to the stone in it (10 to 200). The rock is used up. The elemental's size is the stone divided by 200 (at least a fifth): a small rock gives a small elemental with fewer hit points and weaker blows, but a little faster.
- **Shrinks as it is hurt:** while it has 200 hit points or more, each wound makes it smaller, weaker and quicker.
- **Lasts** until it is killed or its druid dies; then it falls back into small rocks (10 stone each) you can mine.
- While it lives, its druid's charge refills a third slower; that comes back when the elemental dies.
- It takes only a quarter of an arrow's damage, and can wade through water.

## Strong against

- **Almost everything hand to hand.** Through armour 30, a [Knight](knight.md) or [Spearman](spearman.md) does nothing at all, a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) 5. It hits a knight for 88.
- **Missiles**: arrows and bullets do 1 or nothing; a [Mangonel](mangonel.md) stone 1.
- **Buildings**: its blows wreck walls and towers.

## Weak against

- **Exorcism**: a [Priest](priest.md) does 100 to it.
- **Rockets**, which ignore armour, and [Trebuchet](trebuchet.md) stones (45).
- **The heaviest blows**: the [Archangel](michael.md) does 45, an [Imperial War Elephant](war-elephant.md) 20, and blessed or upgraded heavy infantry gets through.
- **Speed**: at 2 everything can walk away from it.

## Tips

- Very slow: wake it where the fight will come to it, or next to the walls you want down.
- Use the biggest rocks you can find: a full-size elemental is far tougher.
- Keep the druid safe: if he dies, the elemental crumbles.
