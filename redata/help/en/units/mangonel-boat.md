## Role

The Carrack is a warship with a catapult: 600 hit points, armour 19 all round and 35-point stones (25 to buildings) about every 8 seconds, from 15 m out to 150 m. It is built at the [Dock](../buildings/dock-wood.md) for 600 wood and 70 gold. It carries no passengers.

## Abilities

- **[Fire (Mangonel)](../commands/fire-mangonel.md):** it never runs out of stones. Every stone also hurts all units within 8 m of where it lands, yours and your allies' included, by a tenth of its damage less each one's armour.
- **[Repair (Docking)](../commands/docking.md):** at your own or an ally's Dock, a damaged Carrack is mended by 10 hit points a second, for 1 wood each, until it is whole.

## Strong against

- **Buildings and troops along the shore**, from 150 m, beyond most towers.
- **Archers ashore**: arrows do little through armour 19.

## Weak against

- **Enemy fire from close by**: it can't hit anything nearer than 15 m.
- It is a poor fighter alongside: its own melee blow is 20.

## Tips

- Use it to batter buildings along the shore from out of their reach.
- Sail it back to a Dock to repair between attacks.
- Mind the splash when your own troops fight on the beach.
