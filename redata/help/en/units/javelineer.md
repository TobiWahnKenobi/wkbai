## Role

The javelineer is a fast mounted skirmisher, speed 13, the fastest regular unit. It throws 13-point javelins every 4 seconds out to 45 m. It has 90 hit points and almost no armour (1 at the front). It costs 90 food and 20 wood at the [Stables](../buildings/stables.md).

## Abilities

- **[Throw Javelin](../commands/fire-javelin.md):** 13 damage, one every 4 s, range 45 m. It lobs over cover when it cannot see the target.
- **Ammunition:** 10 javelins. Ammunition comes back by itself at about 1 point a second, and faster within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](supply-wagon.md) (50 m after [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- **[Skirmish](../commands/skirmish-mode.md):** keeps 27 m from approaching enemies.
- **[Take Cart](../commands/take-cart.md).**
- Javelins count as arrows: arrow armour stops three quarters of their damage.
- It runs away from [Abaddon](abaddon.md) and the [Behemoth](behemoth.md) when idle.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Slow troops it can outrun.** The game rates it well against heavy infantry, heavy cavalry and siege engines: they cannot catch it, and it shoots their backs. A javelin does 12 to a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md)'s rear and to a knight's rear.
- **Peasants, archers and gunners**: 11-12 a javelin, and they cannot escape it.

## Weak against

- **Armour from the front**: 1 to a knight's or a pikeman's front.
- **Archers and gunners shooting back**: an arrow does it 14, a bullet 19.
- **Anything that catches it**: a knight does 28 a blow. Elementals are rated well against it.
- **Arrow-armoured targets** (engines, clerics, monsters): almost nothing.

## Tips

- Use its speed to harass archers, peasants and engines, then ride off before the spears arrive.
- Circle round to shoot at flanks and rears.
- Never charge it into a formed line.
