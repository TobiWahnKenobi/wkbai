## Role

The javelineer is a fast mounted skirmisher (speed 13, the fastest regular unit) that throws javelins for 13 damage. It costs 90 food and 20 wood at the [Stables](../buildings/stables.md).

## Tips

- Use its speed to harass archers, peasants and engines, then ride off before the spears arrive.
- It has almost no armour: never charge it into a formed line.
