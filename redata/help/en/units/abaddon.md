## Role

Abaddon is the demon lord of the pure Pagan path, the Pagan answer to the [Archangel](michael.md). He comes out of [The Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md) after [Summon Abaddon](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-abaddon.md): 800 hit points, armour 25 at the front and 15 on the other sides, 100-point blows and 150 damage to buildings per blow. He is slow (speed 4), cannot be healed, possessed or carried on ships, and you can only have one at a time.

## Abilities

- **He fights on his own.** Once he appears he forms his own army and attacks enemies within 100 m without orders. He goes for religious buildings first (Cathedral, Church, Holy Chapter, Scriptorium, Monastery), then everything else. [Abaddon Attack Player Of Target](../commands/abaddon-attack-player-of-target.md) chooses which enemy player he goes after.
- **Sweeping blows.** Each blow also hits up to 4 other enemy units within 5 m of his victim, for the full damage.
- **Stamp.** When his charge bar is full (80 points, about 76 s to fill) and enemies are in sight, he stamps: every enemy unit and building within 15 m takes 16 points for each metre inside that circle, up to 240 right next to him. Armour does not count. The bar then empties.
- **Burns unbelievers.** The first time he sees an enemy unit, a bolt hits it for 85. If that kills it, the victim bursts into flames.
- **Terror.** Peasants, and idle Militia, Spearmen, Scouts, Barbarians, Mercenaries, Merchants, Javelineers, Squires and Battle Riders that see him run away. Each of them can be scared by him again only after 60 s.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Everything on foot or horseback.** 100 a blow kills an archer or a peasant at once and a knight in two (88 a blow), and the sweep hits the men around them too.
- **Arrows and javelins.** He has armour against small missiles: arrows and javelins do only a quarter of their damage, and against his armour of 15-25 that is nothing at all.
- **Religious buildings and towns.** 150 structure damage per blow, plus the stamp.

## Weak against

- **Holy power.** An [Inquisitor](inquisitor.md)'s [Exorcise Demons](../commands/exorcism.md) does up to 200 damage to a demon, and his [Holy Flame](../commands/holy-fire.md) does double damage to demons (60 before armour, so 35 a shot).
- **The [Archangel](michael.md)** (75 a blow, 50 after armour) and heavy engines. A [Bombard](bombard.md) ball still does 20, a gunner's bullet only 1.
- **Slowness and size.** At speed 4 he cannot catch cavalry or chase archers, and missiles keep chipping at him.

## Tips

- Send him at the enemy's churches and cathedral, and let your army follow behind his stamp.
- Keep him away from inquisitors: kill them first with your own troops.
- Against Abaddon, do not waste arrows. Use gunners, bombards, inquisitors and heavy infantry from the sides, and keep peasants out of his sight.
