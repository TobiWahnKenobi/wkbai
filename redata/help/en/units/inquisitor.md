## Role

The inquisitor is the Imperial witch hunter from the [Cathedral](../buildings/cathedral-stone.md). It burns heretics, banishes enemy magic users, drives out possession and calls down holy fire on enemies at range.

## Trained at / Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->

<!-- /gen:trained -->
