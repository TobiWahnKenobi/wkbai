## Role

The Squire is the first horseman you can train, with no research: 150 hit points, a 25-point blow every 2.5 seconds, armour 8 from the front (4 on the sides, 1 behind) and speed 8.5, for 90 food and 20 wood at the [Stables](../buildings/stables.md). It is heavy cavalry: it speeds up by half over the last 10 m of a charge.

## Strong against

- **Archers and gunners**: 23 a blow, so an [Archer](archer.md) (50 hit points) lasts three blows. Heavy cavalry rates light infantry as one of its best targets.
- **Siege engines**: 20 a blow to a [Mangonel](mangonel.md) or [Trebuchet](trebuchet.md), and they can't run from it.
- **Peasants, merchants and raiders.**

## Weak against

- **Spears and pikes from the front**: a [Spearman](spearman.md) does 22 a blow, a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) 27, and a formed line makes it break off after one blow unless it is in a Wedge.
- **Better cavalry**: a [Knight](knight.md) does 21 a blow and takes 13.
- **Missiles**: an [Archer](archer.md) does 7 a shot through the front, a [Dragoon](mounted-hand-gunner.md) 12.
- The [Archangel](michael.md) and similar demons make idle squires flee.

## Tips

- Fast enough to run down archers and catch raiders.
- Charge in a [Wedge](../commands/formation.md): its first blow does three times the damage, and a Wedge doesn't bounce off pikes.
- Do not charge it into pikes or spears from the front; go round to the flank.
