## Role

The trebuchet is a huge siege catapult: 75 damage, 65 of it to buildings, every 12 seconds, at ranges from 20 to 140. A [Sapper](sapper.md) builds it for 200 food and 500 wood after the [Trebuchet](../upgrades/trebuchet.md) research at the Observatory.

## Tips

- Its range lets it destroy towers and forts without coming within their reach.
- It must be packed up to move, and it is very slow: build it where you mean to use it.
