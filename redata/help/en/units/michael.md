## Role

The Archangel is the champion of the pure Imperial path. Build the [Statue of the Archangel](../buildings/michael-statue.md) (1000 food and 300 gold) and make the [Sacrifice for Archangel](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-michael.md) there: it takes 600 seconds, less if priests [pray](../commands/pray-for-righteousness.md) at it. Then the statue gives way and the Archangel appears, and your enemies are warned. You can have only one at a time; if he dies you can make the sacrifice again.

He has 1000 hit points, armour 25 at the front and 13 elsewhere, and 75-point blows that do 150 damage to buildings. He eats nothing and can't be healed.

## Abilities

- **He fights on his own.** You can select him, but he can't be moved. He goes after enemy temples and Pagan buildings first, then every enemy unit and building. His only command, [Michael Attack Player Of Target](../commands/michael-attack-player-of-target.md), tells him which enemy player to go after: point at anything of that player's.
- **Lightning:** whenever his charge bar (150, refilling by about 1 a second) is full and an enemy is in sight, he strikes at the biggest enemy he can see. Five strikes fall in a line 12 to 60 m in front of him, each hitting everything within 10 m for 75 hit points and 100 structure. The strikes don't pick sides, so keep your own troops out of the line.
- **Lifts curses** from allies in his sight every 21 seconds.
- **Terror:** peasants he sees, and idle light troops (spearmen, squires, scouts, mercenaries, militia, merchants and the like), flee from him.
- He takes only a quarter of an arrow's damage.

## Strong against

- **Everything that fights hand to hand.** He hits a [Knight](knight.md) for 63 and a [Spearman](spearman.md) for 67 (two blows); a knight does 4 a blow to his front.
- **Archers**: arrows do nothing through his armour.
- **Buildings**: 150 structure a blow, plus the lightning.

## Weak against

- **Rockets**, which ignore armour (a direct hit takes 360).
- **Heavy siege**: a [Trebuchet](trebuchet.md) stone does 50.
- **Giants**: a [Stone Elemental](stone-elemental-large.md) or [Abaddon](abaddon.md) does 75 a blow.
- **Many attackers on his flanks**: armour 13 there, so knights do 16 a blow.

## Tips

- One Archangel can break an enemy town on his own; send troops with him to deal with the small fry, out of the lightning's path.
- Point him at the most dangerous enemy with [Michael Attack Player Of Target](../commands/michael-attack-player-of-target.md).
- Fighting him: bring rocket launchers, trebuchets or giants, and attack his sides.
