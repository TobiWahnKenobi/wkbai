## Role

The monastic knight is a holy warrior from the [Holy Chapter](../buildings/holy-chapter.md): 180 hit points, armour 16 from the front and a 37-point attack. It can also heal and drive out evil spirits.

## Trained at / Built by

<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Holy Chapter](../buildings/holy-chapter.md)
- Imperial or neutral side only: the button goes once you build a [Maypole](../buildings/maypole.md) (the Pagan path).
<!-- /gen:needs -->
