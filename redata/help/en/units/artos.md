## Role

Baron Artos is a hero of the campaign: 350 hit points, armour 22 at the front, 13 at the sides and 7 at the rear, and a 45-point blow every second. He is not trained; the missions bring him in.

## Abilities

- **Heals himself.** When he is idle, hurt and no enemy is within 30 m, he spends 10 points of his charge bar (60 at most) each second to heal himself, then moves on to heal other hurt units within 30 m. The charge bar refills by itself at about 1 point a second. Each heal restores 15 hit points, 25 after [Surgery](../upgrades/surgery.md).
- **Heals the unit he guards.** Told to [guard](../commands/guard.md) a unit, he heals it a little every 3 s.
- **Fizzle:** enemy curses, entangles and possessions aimed at units within 50 m of him fail.
- **Warning.** When he is attacked, a yellow circle flashes on the minimap.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Cavalry.** A blow every second for 45: 33 against a knight's front, 40 against its flank. His front armour of 22 turns a knight's blow to 7.
- **Archers and light troops.** His armour stops most of their damage: an arrow does him 1.

## Weak against

- **Heavy infantry in numbers.** A [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) still does 13 a blow and an [Imperial Pikeman](man-at-arms.md) 16, and three at a time wear him down. The game rates heavy infantry as good against him.
- **Being surrounded.** His rear armour is only 7.

## Tips

- He cannot be trained again: keep him out of the worst fighting.
- Pull him out of the fight to let him heal: he heals only when no enemy is within 30 m.
