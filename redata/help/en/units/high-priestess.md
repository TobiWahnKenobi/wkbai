## Role

The high priestess is the Pagan cleric from the [Temple](../buildings/temple.md). She builds the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md), summons the Gibbering Horde, curses the enemy, opens dark portals and can raise a ghost army.

## Trained at / Built by

- **Trained at:** [Temple](../buildings/temple.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Tips

- A high priestess is needed for a Henge: train one early on the Pagan path.
- [Dark Portal](../commands/dark-portal.md) opens a passage that your troops can walk through to another place.
- Pagan side only.
