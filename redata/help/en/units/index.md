# Units

Every unit you can train, build or call up in a normal game, grouped by what it does. Each page has the unit's numbers (in the game they are read live from the game set, so a mod's changes show), where it comes from, what it needs and the orders it takes.

![A mixed army: spearmen in front, archers behind, horsemen on the flank](shot:gamedata-army)

**Reading the numbers:**
- **Armour** is given as a general value plus front, side and rear values: a unit hit from behind takes far more damage than one hit from the front, so flanking pays.
- **Food upkeep** is what the unit eats; your farms decide how much upkeep you can carry.
- **Speed** and **sight** are in the game's own units: compare them between pages.

**Alignment.** Your first religious building decides your side for the rest of the game. A [Church](../buildings/church-stone.md) takes you down the Imperial path, a [Maypole](../buildings/maypole.md) down the Pagan path; staying with neither keeps the Renaissance (technology) path open. Each unit page says which sides can have it.
