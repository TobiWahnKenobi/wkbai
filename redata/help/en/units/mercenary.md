## Role

The mercenary is paid in gold only (20 gold at the [Tavern](../buildings/tavern-wood.md)) and needs no food upkeep, which makes it useful when food is short. It has 120 hit points and armour 10 from the front.

## Tips

- Hire mercenaries when your farms cannot feed more soldiers.
- They are slow (speed 3) and see little (sight 3): pair them with faster units that spot for them.
