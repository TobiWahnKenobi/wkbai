## Role

The Huntress of the Sun is the Pagan archer after the Wickerman's battle gathering: 70 hit points, a 24-point arrow every 4 seconds out to 70 m, a little more armour at the front (4) and speed 5.5, for the same 100 food and 50 wood. When [The Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md) is finished, every [Huntress of the Dawn](pagan-archer.md) you own turns into one, even those in buildings or on ships, and the old button disappears.

## Abilities

- **[Fire Arrow](../commands/fire-arrow.md):** 10 arrows on a full charge bar, refilling by about 1 a second; lobbed over obstacles when she can't see the target.
- **[Skirmish](../commands/skirmish-mode.md):** she steps back from enemy heavy troops within about 35 m, shooting as she goes. [Hold Ground](../commands/hold-ground.md) switches this off.

## Strong against

- **Cavalry**: 12 a shot through a [Knight](knight.md)'s front, 23 from behind.
- **Spearmen and light foot**: 16 a shot to a [Spearman](spearman.md)'s front, 22 to an [Archer](archer.md).

## Weak against

- **Heavy cavalry that reaches her**: a [Knight](knight.md) does 25 a blow.
- **Arrow-proof units**: siege engines, priests and the [Archangel](michael.md) take only a quarter of an arrow's damage; a [Mangonel](mangonel.md)'s front takes 1.
- **Armoured fronts**: 6 a shot to a [Warrior of the Sun](pagan-pikeman.md).

## Tips

- Use her like any archer, behind a wall of spearwomen or pikemen.
- Mass them: a dozen arrows a volley wear down even knights.
- Leave enemy siege engines to cavalry or giants.
