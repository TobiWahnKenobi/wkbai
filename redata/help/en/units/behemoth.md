## Role

The behemoth is the giant monster of the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md): 700 hit points, armour 25 on every side, 150-point blows that reach 8 m, and 70 damage to buildings per blow. It is slow (speed 2.8) and strikes once every 4 seconds. It costs 1000 food and 50 gold.

## Abilities

- **Sweeping blows.** Each blow also hits up to 4 other enemy units within 5 m of its victim, for the full damage.
- **Terror.** Peasants, and idle Militia, Spearmen, Scouts, Barbarians, Mercenaries, Javelineers, Squires and Battle Riders that see it, run away.
- **Arrow armour.** Arrows and javelins do only a quarter of their damage.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Packed infantry and formations.** 150 a blow kills a light soldier in one blow and most heavy ones in two, and the sweep hits up to 4 more each time.
- **Buildings and walls**: 70 structure damage a blow.
- **Archers**: their arrows do nothing against armour 25 with its arrow armour.

## Weak against

- **Cavalry and anything fast.** One blow every 4 s at speed 2.8: riders get away, and the game rates every kind of cavalry well against it.
- **Siege engines and holy fire.** A [Trebuchet](trebuchet.md) stone does 50, a [Bombard](bombard.md) ball 20, an [Inquisitor](inquisitor.md)'s holy flame 35 (double against demons) and his Exorcise Demons up to 200.
- **Heavy infantry in numbers.** A [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) does 10 and an [Imperial Pikeman](man-at-arms.md) 13 a blow, and three can hit it at once.

## Tips

- Walk it into walls, gatehouses and dense formations; that is where the sweep pays.
- Escort it with fast troops to drive off the cavalry and inquisitors that hunt it.
- Against a behemoth, do not crowd it: spread out and use engines and clerics.
