## Role

The horde spewer is a fast Pagan monster (speed 10, 500 hit points) that spews acid at range. It comes from the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md) once you have an [Observatory](../buildings/observatory.md) or a [The Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md).

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->

- Pagan or neutral side only: the button goes once you build a [Church](../buildings/church-stone.md) (the Imperial path).
