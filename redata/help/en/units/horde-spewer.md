## Role

The Spider Demon is a fast Pagan monster from the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md): speed 10, 500 hit points, and an acid spray that burns everything in front of it. It has almost no armour (2 at the front, 0 elsewhere) and no hand-to-hand attack.

## Abilities

- **[Acid Spray](../commands/spew-acid.md):** every enemy unit and building in a cone 45° wide and 35 m long in front of it takes 30 hit points and 50 structure damage. Armour does not count. It sprays from about 30 m.
- **Acid supply:** a charge bar of 150 that starts full. Each spray costs 25, so 6 sprays from full. It refills by itself at about 1 point a second, and a [Supply Wagon](supply-wagon.md) speeds that up.
- **[Hold Fire](../commands/hold-fire.md) / [Fire at Will](../commands/fire-at-will.md)** switch automatic spraying off and on.
- **Demonic:** holy fire does it double damage and [Exorcise Demons](../commands/exorcism.md) works on it. It cannot be turned into a tree by Greengrow.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Packed troops.** 30 to every man in the cone, through any armour: two sprays kill a block of archers or gunners (50 hit points).
- **Heavy infantry, siege engines and buildings.** Its flat damage ignores their armour, and 50 structure a spray hurts houses and walls. The game rates it well against heavy infantry, cavalry and siege engines.

## Weak against

- **Elementals**: the game rates them well against it, and they shrug off the rest of your army.
- **Anything that reaches it.** With armour 2, an arrow does it 13 and a knight 27. Once its acid is spent, it cannot fight back.
- **[Inquisitors](inquisitor.md)**: 58 a holy flame and up to 200 for an exorcism.

## Tips

- Use its speed: run in, spray a dense formation or a block of archers, and run out.
- Watch the charge bar; with less than 25 it is helpless.
- Against it, spread your troops and send cavalry while it is out of acid.
