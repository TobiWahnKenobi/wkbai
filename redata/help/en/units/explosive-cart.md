## Role

The explosive cart is a moving bomb: drive it next to a target and [trigger it](../commands/trigger-explosives.md). It costs 200 food and 50 gold at the [Armoury](../buildings/armoury-stone.md) and needs an [Observatory](../buildings/observatory.md).
