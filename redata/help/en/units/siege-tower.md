## Role

The siege tower carries troops over a wall. A [Sapper](sapper.md) builds it for 200 wood; it has 600 hit points. Push it against an enemy wall, open it, and foot soldiers climb across with [Scale Siege Tower](../commands/scale-siege-tower.md).

## Trained at / Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Built by:** [Sapper](sapper.md) (the engine is put up on the spot like a building, then rolls out)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

- Clear the wall's defenders with missile troops before you send men over.
- [Close Siege Tower](../commands/close-siege-tower.md) before moving it again.
