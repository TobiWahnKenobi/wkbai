## Role

The gibbering horde is a cheap Pagan creature from the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md): 30 food, 40 hit points, armour 4 at the front and 0 at the rear, but a 30-point blow every 2.5 seconds. A [High Priestess](high-priestess.md) can also turn an enemy unit into a pack of them. Hordes multiply by eating the land.

## Abilities

- **[Devour](../commands/devour.md):** the horde eats a tree, a farm, a rock or a gold deposit, a little every 3 s. Gold is worth the most, then farms and rock, then trees. At 40 points (the charge bar shows them), and if you are below your population limit, a new horde splits off and goes to eat the nearest food within 100 m. Eating does not heal it.
- **Demonic:** holy fire does it double damage and [Exorcise Demons](../commands/exorcism.md) works on it.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Cheap damage.** 30 a blow: 28 against an archer, 18 against a knight's front, 29 against its rear.
- **Siege engines and isolated cavalry**: the game rates it well against both.
- **The enemy economy**: hordes left alone eat farms, forests and mines and keep multiplying.

## Weak against

- **Everything that hits it.** 40 hit points: a knight kills it in two blows (25 each), an arrow does 11.
- **Light cavalry**: javelin riders and mounted archers shoot it down; the game rates them well against it.
- **[Inquisitors](inquisitor.md)**: holy flame does 56 to it.

## Tips

- Throw hordes at the enemy in numbers to soak up his attacks while your real army moves in.
- Set a few to devour gold or farms near your base to breed more for free.
- Against hordes, hunt the ones eating your land early, before they split.
