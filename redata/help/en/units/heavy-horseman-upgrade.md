## Role

The War Rider is the [Battle Rider](heavy-horseman.md) after the Wickerman's gathering: when you finish [The Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md), every Battle Rider you have becomes a War Rider, and new ones arrive as War Riders. It has 180 hit points instead of 150, armour 14 at the front, 7 at the sides and 2 at the rear, and a 32-point blow, at the same speed of 8.5.

## Abilities

- **Wedge.** In a [Wedge](../mechanics/formations.md) each rider's first blow does three times the damage.
- **Breaking off.** A rider that strikes formed heavy infantry from the front, when not in a Wedge, breaks off after the blow and rides 25-35 m away.
- No other special abilities.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Missile troops.** 30 a blow to an archer or a gunner. Arrows do it 1 from the front, bullets 6.
- **Siege engines**: 27 a blow to a mangonel.
- **Flanks and rears of infantry**: 26 into a [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md)'s flank, 31 into his rear.

## Weak against

- **Pikes and spears from the front.** A [Man-at-Arms](pikeman.md) does it 21, an [Imperial Pikeman](man-at-arms.md) 24, and it breaks off after one blow from the front unless it is in a Wedge.
- **Light cavalry**: rated well against it.
- **Long melees**: rear armour 2.

## Tips

- Use it like the Battle Rider, as the hammer of a Pagan army: hit flanks and archers, in a Wedge.
- Its front armour makes it good at riding through arrow fire to reach the archers.
