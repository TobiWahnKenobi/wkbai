## Role

The Imperial Archer is the Imperial path's elite bowman: 70 hit points, armour 5 at the front, 20-point arrows and the longest reach of any archer, 125 m. It shoots slowly, about once every 6 seconds. It costs 100 food and 20 gold and needs a [Statue of the Archangel](../buildings/michael-statue.md).

## Abilities

- **[Fire Arrow](../commands/fire-arrow.md):** 20 damage, about one arrow every 6 s, range 125 m. It lobs over walls and hills when it cannot see its target.
- **Ammunition:** 10 arrows. Ammunition comes back by itself at about 1 point a second, and faster within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](supply-wagon.md) (50 m after [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- **[Skirmish](../commands/skirmish-mode.md):** steps back to 40 m from approaching enemies.
- **[Hunt Missile](../commands/hunt-missile.md):** shoots sheep.

## Strong against

Damage numbers below are per blow or per shot, before research, experience, height and formation bonuses: the attack minus the armour on the side that is hit (see [Combat](../mechanics/combat.md)).

- **Anything slow, from far away.** At 125 m it outranges every other archer and gunner.
- **Unarmoured troops**: 18 a shot to a gunner or an archer, 19 to a peasant.
- **Cavalry from the side or behind**: 15 into a knight's flank, 19 into its rear, against 8 at the front.

## Weak against

- **Anything with arrow armour** (siege engines, clerics, elementals, the Behemoth, Abaddon): a quarter of 20 against their armour does nothing.
- **Heavy cavalry that reaches it.** A knight does 24 a blow; heavy cavalry is rated well against all archers.
- **Heavily armoured fronts**: an [Imperial Pikeman](man-at-arms.md) takes 5, a [Warrior of the Sun](pagan-pikeman.md) 2.

## Tips

- Its long sight lets it pick targets before the enemy sees it: shoot, then step back.
- It shoots slowly, so it pays off against targets that cannot reach it.
- Keep pikes in front of it against cavalry.
