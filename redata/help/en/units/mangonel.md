## Role

The mangonel is the first siege catapult you can train, with no research: 30 damage (40 to buildings) every 7 seconds at ranges from 15 to 90. It costs 200 food and 200 wood at the [Armoury](../buildings/armoury-stone.md).

## Tips

- Keep it behind your troops: it cannot hit anything closer than 15.
- Good against towers, walls and dense groups of foot.
