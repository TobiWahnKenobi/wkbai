## Role

The Pagan archer shoots harder than the common archer (20 damage) and has a little more health (60), for 100 food and 50 wood at the [Hall of Warriors](../buildings/hall-of-warriors.md).

## Trained at / Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Trained at:** [Hall of Warriors](../buildings/hall-of-warriors.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Hall of Warriors](../buildings/hall-of-warriors.md) or building a [Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md)
- Pagan or neutral side only: the button goes once you build a [Church](../buildings/church-stone.md) (the Imperial path).
<!-- /gen:needs -->
