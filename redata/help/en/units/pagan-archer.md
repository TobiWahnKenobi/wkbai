## Role

The Huntress of the Dawn is the Pagan archer: a 20-point arrow every 4 seconds out to 70 m and 60 hit points, for 100 food and 50 wood at the [Hall of Warriors](../buildings/hall-of-warriors.md). She is light infantry with almost no armour (3 at the front, 1 on the sides, none behind).

## Abilities

- **[Fire Arrow](../commands/fire-arrow.md):** 10 arrows on a full charge bar, refilling by about 1 a second. Without a clear view she lobs the arrow over the obstacle, less accurately.
- **[Skirmish](../commands/skirmish-mode.md):** she steps back from enemy heavy infantry, heavy cavalry or a fighting siege beast within about 35 m, shooting as she goes (not in a formation, nor with your heavy infantry close in front). [Hold Ground](../commands/hold-ground.md) switches this off.

## Strong against

- **Light troops**: 18 a shot to an [Archer](archer.md), 19 to a peasant.
- **Cavalry from the side or behind**: a [Knight](knight.md) takes 15 from the side and 19 from behind (8 from the front).
- **Spearmen**: 12 a shot through a [Spearman](spearman.md)'s front.

## Weak against

- **Heavy cavalry that reaches her**: heavy cavalry rates light infantry as one of its best targets; a [Knight](knight.md) does 26 a blow and kills her in three.
- **Arrow-proof units**: siege engines, priests, the [Archangel](michael.md) and the giants take a quarter of an arrow's damage, which their armour usually swallows whole.
- **Armoured fronts**: 2 a shot to a [Warrior of the Sun](pagan-pikeman.md), 1 to the [Undead Legion](undead-legion.md).

## Tips

- Keep her behind a line of spearwomen or pikemen and let Skirmish pull her back.
- Shoot from high ground: each metre above the target adds 5% damage.
- Building [The Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md) turns all your Huntresses of the Dawn into the stronger [Huntress of the Sun](pagan-archer-upgrade.md).
