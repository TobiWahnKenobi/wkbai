# Interface

What the in-game screen shows over the battlefield. All four are on by default.

![The Interface page](shot:options-interface)

## Show hero names

Heroes (units that have made a name for themselves in battle) carry their name over their heads, in their player's colour, fading with distance and hidden in the fog of war. Off: no names over units.

## Show unit names on hover

The unit under the mouse gets a one-line tooltip with its name, its type and its kills. Off: no tooltip.

## Timeline (F7) told as a chronicle

The timeline window (F7) opens on its chronicle: the game's battles, the settlements founded and razed and the fall of heroes, told as a historian's journal, oldest first. Off: it opens on the statistics, newest first. The window's own Chronicle and Statistics buttons switch between the two and set this too.

## Building health bars

A bar over each building you have selected or that is under the mouse shows its state: full and green when unharmed, through yellow to red as it is damaged. Off: no bars.

> wkbai: building health bars come from Warrior Kings' manual; hero names, the tooltips and the chronicle are wkbai's own [unit history](help:mechanic/unit-history).
