# Picture

The colours of the 3D scene, as on a television. The interface (menus, panels, text) isn't changed by them. **Cost:** none.

![Graphics > Picture](shot:options-picture)

## Exposure

How much light the picture takes in, as with a camera: 0.25 to 4, default 1. Values below 1 darken the scene, values above 1 brighten it.

## Brightness

Lifts or lowers every colour by the same amount: -0.5 to 0.5, default 0.

## Contrast

Spreads the colours apart from the middle grey, or draws them together: 0.25 to 2, default 1.

## Saturation

How strong the colours are: 0 (black and white) to 2 (twice as strong), default 1.

> wkbai: the original has no picture controls; these are wkbai's own. At their defaults the colours are unchanged.
