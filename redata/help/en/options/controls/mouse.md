# Mouse

![Controls > Mouse](shot:options-mouse)

## Camera rotation speed (middle button)

How fast the camera turns while you hold the middle mouse button and move the mouse: 0.001 to 0.050, default 0.010 (in radians per pixel the mouse moves, so 0.010 turns the camera about half a degree per pixel).

## Invert mouse wheel

Off by default. On: the mouse wheel zooms the other way round.

## Scroll at the window's edge

Off by default. On: the view scrolls while the mouse rests at an edge of the window, at the [scroll speed](camera.md#scroll-speed).

## Single click selects all units

On by default, as in the original. On: for the units the game marks for it (soldiers, mostly), a single click selects every unit of that type nearby and a double click selects just the one; for all other units it is the other way round. Off: a single click always selects one unit, and a double click all of its type nearby.

> wkbai: Single click selects all units is the original's setting. The others are wkbai's own.
