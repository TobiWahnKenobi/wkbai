# Controls

The Controls tab has three pages:

- **[Keyboard](keyboard.md):** every key of the game, two keys per action, all changeable.
- **[Mouse](mouse.md):** turning the camera with the middle button, the wheel, scrolling at the window's edge, how clicks select.
- **[Camera](camera.md):** scroll speeds, how low and high the camera goes, the fixed camera, the minimap's camera jump.

How to use the mouse and the keys in the game is explained under [Controls](help:controls).
