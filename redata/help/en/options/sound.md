# Sound

Music, the sound effects, and how the world's sounds are placed around you.

![The Sound page](shot:options-sound)

## Music

The first switch on the page: **On** (the default) plays music in the menus and in the game; **Off** plays none.

## Music volume

How loud the music is, 0 to 100 %, default 100 %. Applied when you press OK.

## Effects volume

How loud everything else is (units, buildings, battle, weather, the interface), 0 to 100 %, default 100 %. Applied when you press OK.

## 3D Sound

On by default, as in the original. On: the world's sounds come from where they happen, left or right of the camera. Off: they all play from the middle, without direction, though still quieter the further away they are.

## Headphone 3D sound (HRTF)

Off by default. On: sounds are shaped the way your ears hear them, so that with headphones they seem to come from in front, behind, above or below. Through speakers it sounds dull; leave it off then.

## Reverb

Off by default. On: the world's sounds echo softly as over an open plain, and distant sounds lose their high notes. The music and the interface's sounds are not changed.

> wkbai: Music, the volumes and 3D Sound are the original's settings. Headphone 3D sound and Reverb are wkbai's own, from its sound library (OpenAL Soft).
