# Using the help

## Help for anything on screen

- **Right-click > Help:** right-click any button, list, switch or panel and choose **Help**. The help opens at that thing's own entry.
- **Alt+right-click:** the same menu on parts where a right-click already does something: train and build buttons (a right-click takes one off the queue), the minimap and the battlefield (a right-click gives orders). Alt+right-click on a unit or building opens its page.
- **F1:** help for whatever is under the cursor. Over nothing, it opens the help for the screen you are on. (F1 is the server window's cheat key today; with the help viewer that moves to Ctrl+F1.)
- **"?" buttons:** the in-game menu, the options and every settings window have one in their title bar.

## What's this?

Press **Shift+F1** and the cursor turns into a question mark. The next click opens the help for what you click on and does nothing else. Esc leaves this mode.

## The help window

- **Contents** on the left, the page on the right.
- **Search** at the top finds words in titles, headings and text; titles come first.
- **Back, Forward, Home:** the buttons at the top, or the mouse's side buttons while the cursor is over the help window (elsewhere they keep moving the camera).
- **Show me** links flash the part of the screen a page talks about.
- **Live numbers:** the tables of costs, hit points and attack on unit and building pages come from the game you are playing, mods included.
- **Screenshots:** wkbai takes them on your computer from your own copy of the game, the first time you open the help, or when you press **Take screenshots**. Until then a frame with a description stands in their place.
- In single player the game pauses while the help is open (Options can switch that off). In multiplayer it goes on.
