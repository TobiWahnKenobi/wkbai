# Unit history and heroes

> **wkbai.** Names, kills and heroes are wkbai's own. They don't change how a unit fights; they tell its story.

## Names, age and kills

- **Every soldier gets a name** when it is made, drawn from English, French, Norse and Nordic names by its side, unique among your units. Boats get ships' names. Peasants, siege engines, carts, wagons and summoned creatures have none. Named characters (Michael, Abaddon, the campaign heroes) keep their own.
- **Militia** get a name when called up; a peasant who killed as militia keeps it.
- **Kills** are counted from the last blow before an enemy dies. A missile's kill goes to whoever shot it; a tower's or fort's kills go to the troops inside. Buildings destroyed are counted apart. Allies, your own units and animals don't count.
- **Age** is shown as years from 25 with wkbai's seasons on, in days with only day and night on, otherwise as playing time.

The [selection panel](../ui/hud/selection-panel.md) shows the name, age and kills; the hover tooltip shows the name and kills.

## Heroes

- **The threshold:** all players' population limits added together, divided by 10, but at least **50** kills. Two players at 200 need 50; eight at 200 need 160.
- A unit that reaches it becomes a **hero** and gains an epithet in its name's language ("the Valiant").
- **Formations:** a formation makes at most one hero of its own. A member who leaves keeps only half the kills it made in the formation, unless it is a hero.
- Heroes' names can be shown over their heads (an interface option).
- **A hero's fall** is announced and recorded in the [timeline](../ui/hud/chronicle.md), with who slew it.

## The timeline

The battle timeline (F7) lists the game's battles, sieges and raids. Each entry names every side's best fighter in it, and the heroes who were there. It shows only what you saw, unless the game is over for you.
