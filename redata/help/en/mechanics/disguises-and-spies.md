# Disguises and spies

Some units can pass for harmless peasants. To an enemy they look exactly like one of **the enemy's own** peasants, in its colour, on the map and on the minimap, and they have no selection panel for it.

## Who goes in disguise

| Unit | Looks like |
|---|---|
| [Spy](../units/spy.md) | a male peasant |
| [Succubus](../units/succubus.md) | a female peasant at market |
| a peasant taken over by the *Possess* spell | a peasant at market |

You always see your own and your allies' disguised units as they are.

## Seeing through a disguise

- **Holy men and spies see through them.** A [Priest](../units/priest.md), [Bishop](../units/bishop.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md), [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [Spy](../units/spy.md) unmasks an enemy spy it sees. Succubi and possessed peasants are unmasked in the same way by those who can see them for what they are.
- **Towers and forts** unmask spies they see once their owner has the [Espionage](../upgrades/espionage.md) research (at the University), or has a spy of its own garrisoned inside.
- **Caught in the act:** a spy who attacks a fortified building is unmasked to everyone near.
- **Once unmasked**, the spy can be attacked like any unit, and nearby enemies notice it. When no detector sees it any more, it slips back into its disguise.

## What spies do

- **[Arson Attack](../commands/arson.md):** set an enemy building on fire.
- **[Blow Open Gates](../commands/blow-open-gates.md):** open an enemy gatehouse.
- **[Spy on Infantry](../commands/spy-on-infantry.md)** (and on cavalry): show you the enemy's troops for 10 seconds, even in the fog.
- **Ride in a cart:** a spy can hide in a cart and climb out when it delivers in an enemy town.
