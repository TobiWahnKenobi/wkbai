# Glossary

The words this help uses, in alphabetical order, with the page that explains each.

- **Agent control:** a setting that lets a program on your computer play in your place, for tests and fair computer players. Everyone in the game sees when it is on ([agent control](options/agent-control.md)).
- **Aggressive AI:** a lobby setting of wkbai's, on at first: the AI Generals build their army first and attack as soon as they can afford to ([AI opponents](mechanics/ai-opponents.md)).
- **AI General:** a computer player, a named lord with a portrait, a story and a personality ([AI opponents](mechanics/ai-opponents.md), [AI General Editor](ui/frontend/general-editor.md)).
- **Alignment:** the way of life your realm grows into by what it builds: Imperial, Renaissance or Pagan, and two mixed paths ([alignment and the five paths](mechanics/alignment.md)).
- **Archive (`.bcp`):** a file that holds many game files. The game's own is `data.bcp`; mods may bring more ([mods](mechanics/mods.md)).
- **Cheat mode:** a skirmish setting that opens the developer and cheat tools; never in multiplayer ([Cheat mode](ui/frontend/skirmish/weather.md#cheat-mode)).
- **Chronicle:** the timeline (F7) told as a story of your realm's events ([statistics and timeline](ui/hud/chronicle.md)).
- **City:** the walled area that starts at your Manor and grows with every city building; barracks, churches, shops and the like must join it ([buildings and construction](mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md)).
- **Control group:** units kept under a number key, to select them again with one press ([selecting](controls/selection.md#control-groups)).
- **Enhanced renderer:** wkbai's drawing of the game with its own effects, on by default; off, the game is drawn as the original draws it ([graphics](options/graphics/index.md#the-enhanced-renderer)).
- **Experience:** what a unit gains by fighting; it makes the unit stronger ([experience](mechanics/experience.md)).
- **Extend map:** land drawn beyond the map's edge, to look at only ([Terrain and grass](options/graphics/terrain-grass.md#extend-map)).
- **Fog of war:** the parts of the map none of your units sees right now ([fog of war and sight](mechanics/fog-of-war.md)).
- **Food support:** how much army your farmers can feed. An army that eats near or above it takes most of your harvest ([food, population and upkeep](mechanics/population-and-food.md)).
- **Formation:** a block of soldiers of one type that moves and fights together: Line, Column, Orb or Wedge ([formations](mechanics/formations.md)).
- **Game set:** the game's rules, written as scripts: units, buildings, costs, research, the AI. A mod can change it ([mods](mechanics/mods.md)).
- **Garrison:** units inside a building; missile units shoot out of it ([buildings and construction](mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#garrisons)).
- **Guest:** a player who joins a multiplayer game that another player hosts ([joining a game](ui/frontend/multiplayer/guest.md)).
- **Headquarters:** your Manor, and later the Keep, Castle or Palace it becomes ([alignment](mechanics/alignment.md)).
- **Hero:** one of your best fighters, named and remembered by wkbai ([unit history and heroes](mechanics/unit-history.md)).
- **Host:** the player whose computer runs a multiplayer game ([hosting a game](ui/frontend/multiplayer/host.md)).
- **Kingmaker:** the campaign of *Warrior Kings: Battles*, a conquest of 22 provinces; also the name of the game's AI scripts ([the Kingmaker campaign](mechanics/campaign.md)).
- **King Piece:** a Standard-mode setting: each player has a king, and loses when he dies ([victory and defeat](mechanics/victory-conditions.md#king-piece)).
- **Knuckle buttons:** the order buttons on the "knuckles" round the rim of the lower left panel ([command buttons](ui/hud/command-panel.md#knuckle-buttons)).
- **Lua AI:** an AI General played by a Lua script, chosen per position in the lobby ([AI opponents](mechanics/ai-opponents.md)).
- **Map folder (`.wkbai`):** wkbai's own map format, a folder that holds a map, its level and its water; the map editor saves into one ([wkbai's map format](ui/editor/map-format.md)).
- **Ministry AI:** wkbai's lobby setting that runs every AI General as a government of departments ([AI opponents](mechanics/ai-opponents.md)).
- **Mod:** files that add to or replace the game's, such as new rules, maps or art ([mods](mechanics/mods.md), [the Mods screen](ui/frontend/mods.md)).
- **Population Limit:** the most people a player may have ([food, population and upkeep](mechanics/population-and-food.md#the-population-limit)).
- **Recording, replay:** a recorded game, and watching it again ([saves and replays](mechanics/saves-and-replays.md)).
- **Resource Points:** the budget for buying an army in Valhalla mode ([Army Enlistment](ui/frontend/skirmish/army.md)).
- **River currents:** wkbai's lobby setting, with the seasons: moving water carries and hurts the units in it, and they swim to the shore ([river currents and swimming](mechanics/water-and-currents.md)).
- **Siege engine:** a machine that batters buildings and walls, such as the Trebuchet ([siege](mechanics/siege.md)).
- **Skirmish:** a single game against AI Generals on a map of your choice ([your first skirmish](getting-started.md)).
- **Spectator:** someone who watches a multiplayer game without playing ([multiplayer](mechanics/multiplayer.md)).
- **Spoils of War:** what a campaign province gives you for conquering it ([the Kingmaker campaign](mechanics/campaign.md#spoils-of-war)).
- **Standard mode:** the usual game, with an economy, where you lose with your last headquarters ([victory and defeat](mechanics/victory-conditions.md#standard-mode)).
- **Tier:** an AI General's level, Conscript, Veteran or Elite, which sets how large its realm may grow ([AI opponents](mechanics/ai-opponents.md#tiers)).
- **Tribute:** resources sent as a gift to another player ([diplomacy](mechanics/diplomacy.md#tributes)).
- **Upkeep:** the food your soldiers eat, taken from your harvest and your store ([food, population and upkeep](mechanics/population-and-food.md#the-upkeep-penalty)).
- **User folder:** `%APPDATA%\wkbai`, where your saves, replays and campaigns are kept ([files and folders](files.md#your-own-folder)).
- **Valhalla mode:** a battle without an economy: armies bought before the start fight for flags and victory points ([victory and defeat](mechanics/victory-conditions.md#valhalla-mode)).
- **Village:** a building that starts a settlement of its own, clear of every city ([Village](buildings/village-wood.md)).
- **Wind:** wkbai's simulated wind, with a direction and a strength that change through the game; set on the lobby's Wind tab ([Wind tab](ui/frontend/skirmish/wind.md), [seasons and weather](mechanics/seasons-weather.md#wind)).
- **Wind chill:** how much colder the wind makes units feel ([seasons and weather](mechanics/seasons-weather.md#wind)).
- **`wkconfig.json`:** wkbai's settings file, in its folder ([settings without a menu](options/config-file.md)).
