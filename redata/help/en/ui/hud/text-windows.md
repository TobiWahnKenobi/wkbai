# Text windows and objectives

## Text windows

![A tutorial's text window with its Back and Next buttons](shot:hud-text-window)

Levels talk to you in framed windows with a gold rim: campaign briefings, the tutorials' steps, quests and warnings. Each level places its own windows. One placed at the bottom of the screen sits between the two bottom panels, or above them when there is no room.

### Pages and buttons

- A window with several pages shows **"2/7"** at its lower right.
- Its buttons are the level's own, usually **Back**, **Next** and **OK** or **Close**.
- A button is greyed where it would do nothing: Back on the first page, Next on the last.
- Some windows must be read to the end: their Close works only on the last page.
- Some buttons also make something happen in the level, such as starting the next step of a tutorial.

### Windows and the mouse

- A window under the mouse takes the clicks; the battlefield under it gets none.
- With several windows, the newest is on top and gets the click first.
- Some windows hold the game's mouse until they are closed (a briefing, for example). The keys still work.
- While the game menu is open, the windows take no clicks.

## Mission objectives

![The objectives window of the Interactive Tutorial](shot:hud-objectives)

Levels with objectives (the tutorials) list them in a window at the upper right, under the top bar. Open and close it with the top bar's [Mission Objectives](top-bar.md#mission-objectives) button or **O**.

- Each objective has a bullet. A **greyed** one, with a grey bullet, is done or no longer counts.
- New objectives make the top-bar button flash until you open the window.
- **OK** closes it.

## AI Generals' messages

![An AI General's message with its portrait](shot:hud-ai-message)

A computer General's message shows in the same frame, with its portrait at the left and its name as the title in the General's colour. The messages queue up: **OK** shows the next.

> wkbai: the original drew these windows the same way; wkbai draws them at a whole-number scale of the original's pixels, so the text stays sharp at any window size. Text windows and objectives aren't kept in a saved game.
