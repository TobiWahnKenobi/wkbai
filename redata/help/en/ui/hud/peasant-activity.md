# Peasant activity

![All five peasant activity tubes above the minimap](shot:hud-peasant-activity)

The top arc of the [minimap](minimap.md#the-four-buttons) opens a column of glass tubes above the minimap that count what your peasants are doing. Click the arc to go through its three steps:

1. **the idle peasants' tube** only;
2. **all five tubes**, framed by two rods;
3. **closed** again.

## The five tubes

Each tube has the activity's icon at its right end, the count in cyan, and a red bar that grows from the right with the count.

| Tube | Counts |
|---|---|
| Shop | Shop keepers + gold miners ("3 + 5") |
| Farm | Farmers |
| Wood | Wood choppers + stone miners |
| Build | Builders (repairers included) |
| Idle | Peasants with nothing to do |

## Selecting by activity

Click a tube's **icon**: the peasants doing that activity become the selection. **Shift-click** adds them to it. The idle tube is the quickest way to find lazy peasants; **I** does the same one at a time ([selection keys](../../controls/selection.md#selection-keys)).
