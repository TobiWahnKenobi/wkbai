# Chat and notices

![Chat lines at the left, the typing line open](shot:hud-chat)

## Chat

In a multiplayer game the last messages show at the left of the screen, a tenth of the way down, in pale gold: up to eight lines, each with the sender's name in front ("Anna: ready?"). A line fades away 15 seconds after it came. In single player there is no chat.

### Typing a message

1. Press **Tab** or **Enter** (key action [Chat](../../controls/keys.md#game)). A "Say:" line opens under the messages, and every recent message shows again while it is open.
2. Type the message.
3. **Enter** sends it to every player; **Esc** closes the line without sending.

While the line is open the game takes no keys: W, Esc and the rest go into the text.

## Notices

Diplomacy news shows in the middle of the screen, under the top bar, in the colour of the player it is about: an offer received or withdrawn, a change of status, a tribute received. Each notice stays 8 seconds and fades over two more.

## Messages from AI Generals

The computer's Generals write to you: threats, demands, offers. Their messages come in a framed window with the General's portrait and name in its colour. One shows at a time; press **OK** for the next. See [Text windows](text-windows.md#ai-generals-messages).
