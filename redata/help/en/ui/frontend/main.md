# Main menu

The first screen after the intro: the Warrior Kings logo over the sunset, and a column of buttons. Each button opens a screen; Exit leaves the game.

![The main menu](shot:frontend-main)

## Load Game

Opens [Load Game](load.md): your saved games, the game's own scenario saves, and the replays of recorded games.

## Campaign

Opens [Campaign](campaign.md), where you start a new Kingmaker campaign or carry on with one, province by province.

## Skirmish

Opens the [skirmish lobby](skirmish/index.md): pick a map, set up the AI Generals and the rules, and play.

## Multiplayer

Opens [Multiplayer](multiplayer/index.md): host a game, join one by its address, or find games on your network and online.

## Tutorial

Opens the [Tutorials](tutorial.md): the interactive tutorial and the four lessons on economy and warfare.

## Options

Opens the [Options](../../options/index.md): graphics, controls and keys, sound, interface, display and online settings.

## Mods

Opens the [mod manager](mods.md), where you switch mods on and off and choose which one wins when two change the same file.

## Map Editor

Opens the [list of maps to edit](map-editor.md). The chosen map opens paused with the editor.

## AI General Editor

Opens the [AI General Editor](general-editor.md), where you make AI Generals of your own with a portrait, a story and fourteen personality sliders.

## Credits

Shows the [credits](credits.md) of the original game and of wkbai.

## Exit

Asks whether you really want to leave, then closes wkbai.

## The wkbai mark

The small "wkbai" in the bottom right corner shows that this is wkbai, not the original game.

> wkbai: Mods and Map Editor are wkbai's own entries. A mod can add more buttons to this column; the column tightens its spacing so that they all fit.
