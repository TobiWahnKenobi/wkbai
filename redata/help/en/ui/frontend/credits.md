# Credits

The names of the people who made Warrior Kings: Battles at Black Cactus and its publisher, followed by wkbai's, scroll slowly up the screen over a dark column. When the last line has passed, the credits start again.

wkbai's licence and the other free software it is built with are on [about wkbai](../../about.md).

![The credits scrolling](shot:frontend-credits)

## Back

Returns to the [main menu](main.md). Esc does the same.

> wkbai: the original's credits are built into its program; wkbai shows the same names in the same order, then adds its own and the statement that wkbai is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Black Cactus, the rights holders of Warrior Kings, wkbre2 or AdrienTD.
