# Tutorials

Five guided games that teach Warrior Kings step by step. Each button starts its level at once; text windows in the game explain what to do next.

![The Tutorials screen](shot:frontend-tutorial)

## Interactive Tutorial

The place to begin: the controls, from the camera to selecting units and giving orders.

## Basic Economy

The first steps of running a town: peasants gathering food, wood and gold.

## Advanced Economy

More of the town's buildings, and how to make it grow.

## Basic Military

Training soldiers and leading them into a fight.

## Advanced Military

More of warfare: larger armies and how to use them.

## Back

Returns to the [main menu](main.md).

## Tips

- In a tutorial, the in-game menu's Restart Level starts the lesson from the beginning.
- A lesson's last page can take you straight to the next lesson, or back to the main menu.

> wkbai: the lessons are the original game's own levels and scripts, run as the original runs them.
