# Joining a game

The guest's lobby: what the host has set up, the chat, and your place in it. The host sets the map and the rules; you choose a position, your colour and your team, and say when you are ready.

![The guest's lobby](shot:frontend-mpguest)

## Connection

The line at the top: "Connecting to ..." while it connects, "Connected as" your name once in, or in red that there is no connection, or that the host turned you away and why (a wrong password, no free place, mods or version that don't fit, a locked game).

## The host's lobby

The map's name, then one row per start position: its number, player, colour, team, and "ready" for each guest who is. Your own row is in orange, the host and other guests in green, AI Generals in yellow, empty positions in grey.

A click on a free position (an AI General's or an empty one) asks the host for it. Taking another position makes you not ready again.

## Your colour and team

In your own row, a click on the colour takes the next colour, a click on the team the next team (1 to 4, or none). They are requests: the host's lobby applies them, and you see them a moment later.

## Joining a running game

When the game has already started, the list shows the places you can take instead: sides an AI General plays, and sides whose player has left. Click one to play it. **Watch the game** (when the host allows spectators) joins as a spectator; the number of free spectator places is shown beside it. "No place is free." means you can only watch, or not join at all.

## Chat

The right half of the screen: the lobby's messages. Type in the line at the bottom and press Enter to send.

## Army Enlistment

In a Valhalla game: opens your [Army Enlistment](../skirmish/army.md) panel in place of the chat; press it again to go back to the chat. Your army is sent to the host as you change it.

## Ready

Tells the host you are ready; it then reads **Not ready**, to take it back. The host can start only when every guest is ready.

## Back

Leaves the game's lobby. Esc does the same.
