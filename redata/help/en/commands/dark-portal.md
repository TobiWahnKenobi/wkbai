# Create Dark Portal

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Create Dark Portal](game:interface/icons/SIC_Portal.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or the [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) turns one of your possessed peasants into a doorway: a portal opens beside the caster and another where the peasant stood, and your units can step from one to the other. It needs an [Arcanum](../buildings/arcanum.md).

## How it works

- **Target:** one of your possessed peasants (see [Possess](possess.md)). Click the button, then the peasant. There is no range limit: the peasant can be deep in enemy land.
- **Effect:** the peasant is used up. One portal opens 10 m in front of the caster, the other where the peasant was. Units you send into the first ([Enter Dark Portal](enter-dark-portal.md)) come out at the second.
- **Duration:** the portals stay open for about two minutes, or until they have carried their fill of units.
- **Cost:** 60 mana. The high priestess's mana bar holds 60, the arch druid's 200; both refill by 2 every 1.9 s (about a minute and about 3 minutes from empty to full), the druid's less while his elementals live. Supply wagons don't refill them.
- **Limits:** one pair of portals per caster at a time.
