# Create Dark Portal

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Create Dark Portal](game:interface/icons/SIC_Portal.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Opens a dark portal: a passage that your units can walk through. It needs an [Arcanum](../buildings/arcanum.md).

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md), [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Arcanum](../buildings/arcanum.md)
<!-- /gen:needs -->
