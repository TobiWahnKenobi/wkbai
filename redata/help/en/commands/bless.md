# Bless

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Bless](game:interface/icons/sic_Bless.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Priest](../units/priest.md) blesses a group of friendly units: for half a minute they hit twice as hard. A blessing also lifts a [Curse](curse.md).

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click a friendly unit (the cursor shows the blessing over a unit you can bless).
- **Target:** one of your own or an allied unit. Buildings can't be blessed.
- **Range:** the priest walks to within 50 m of the target, then raises his hands; the blessing lands at the height of the gesture.
- **Area:** the target and up to seven more friendly units within 20 m of it (the ones nearest the priest).
- **Effect:** each blessed unit does double damage: hand to hand, with missiles, and with missiles against buildings. A sparkle shows on each one.
- **Duration:** 30 s, then the damage goes back to normal.
- **Cost:** 60 mana, a priest's whole bar.
- **Recharge:** a priest starts with no mana and gains 2 every 1.9 s (about 1 a second), so he can bless about once a minute. Supply wagons don't refill it. While the bar is short the button stays grey.
- **Limits:** a unit that is already blessed isn't blessed again: blessings don't stack.
- **Against a Curse:** a cursed unit loses the curse first and gets its full damage back, then it is blessed as well.

## Tips

- Bless just before your troops meet the enemy, so the 30 s are spent fighting.
- Pick a target in the middle of a tight group, so that all eight blessings land.
