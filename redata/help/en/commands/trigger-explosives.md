# Detonate

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Detonate](game:interface/icons/sic_Detonate.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Lights the [Explosive Cart](../units/explosive-cart.md)'s fuse. 30 seconds later the cart blows up, wrecking everything around it.

## How it works

- **How:** press the button. The cart lights its fuse; its bar then counts the 30 s down.
- **While the fuse burns:** the cart speeds up from 5 to 8, so you can still drive it into place.
- **Area and damage:** every unit and building within 30 m, yours and your allies' included, is hit. Armour doesn't help. The damage falls off with distance: at the cart, 480 hit points and 300 structure; at 15 m, 240 and 150; nothing at 30 m.
- **On a ship:** if the cart goes off while aboard, the ship is destroyed and the blast hits everything around it.
- **The cart itself:** it is destroyed in the blast, which also uncovers the land 40 m around for a moment.
- **Limits:** the button is greyed out once the fuse is lit; you can't put it out.
- **Counter:** a cart destroyed before its fuse burns down doesn't explode. A flaming arrow that hits a cart has two chances in three of setting it off at once.
