# Spread Heresy

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Spread Heresy](game:interface/icons/SIC_heritic.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

A possessed peasant (see [Possess](possess.md)) starts preaching. The enemy's peasants nearby stop working and gather round to listen, so their economy stalls while it lasts.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** a button on your possessed peasant; no target. It stands still and preaches.
- **Area:** every enemy [Peasant](../units/peasant.md) within 50 m. Every 5 s it draws in any new ones that come within 50 m.
- **Effect:** the listening peasants leave their work, stand in a ring around the heretic and pray. While they listen, their owner can't select or target them.
- **Duration:** as long as the heretic keeps preaching. When you give it another order, or it dies, the peasants are set free.
- **Cost:** free.
- **Limits:** every 5 s there is a 1 in 4 chance that the heretic's disguise slips for 8 s.
- **Counter:** a [Priest](../units/priest.md) or [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md) can [burn the heretic](burn-heretic.md), and the Pagan casters can [Burn Unbeliever](burn-unbeliever.md). The preaching also fails at once if one of your own (or an ally's) fizzlers, such as your [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md), stands within 50 m of the heretic.

## Tips

- Preach in the middle of the enemy's farms or woodcutters, where many peasants work close together, and keep your own casters away.
