# Close (Gatehouse Ramparts)

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Close (Gatehouse Ramparts)](game:interface/icons/sic_close_gate.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Shuts your open [Gatehouse Ramparts](../buildings/celestial-gatehouse-closed.md), so nobody can pass through it.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select the open gatehouse and click the button.
- **What happens:** the gate plays its closing animation and sound; when that ends, the gatehouse is closed and blocks the way like the wall. Its button is now [Open](open-celestial-gatehouse.md).
- **Limits:** clicking again while it closes does nothing.
- **Counter:** an enemy [Spy](../units/spy.md) can wreck a closed gatehouse with [Blow Open Gates](blow-open-gates.md).
