# Guard Next Unit Selected

## Role

Orders the selected units to guard a unit, a building or a place: they stay close and fight what comes near.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** every unit except [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md), [Spy](../units/spy.md), [The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md), [Trebuchet](../units/trebuchet.md), [Undead Legion](../units/undead-legion.md)
- Hold **G** and right-click a unit or building (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#follow-and-guard)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Guard siege engines and supply wagons on the march.
