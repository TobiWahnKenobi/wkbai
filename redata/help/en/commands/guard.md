# Guard Next Unit Selected

## Role

Your units stay close to a unit, building or formation and fight the enemy soldiers that come near it. Hold **G** and right-click the one to guard; the cursor shows the guard sign over something you may guard.

## How it works

- **Target:** a unit, building or formation of yours or of an ally.
- **Staying close:** each guard follows the guarded at its own spot, up to 10 m to its side and front, so they don't all crowd onto it.
- **Range:** about every 3 seconds the guards look for enemy soldiers within 120 m of the one they guard and attack them. Guards that chase too far (beyond 180 m) are called back.
- **Soldiers only:** they go after enemy units, not enemy buildings.
- **Helpers in the group:** a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) heals the guarded and allied soldiers within 15 m of it when they are hurt; the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md) and Artos heal them too. A [Priest](../units/priest.md) casts [Bless](bless.md) and an [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) casts [Barkskin](barkskin.md) on the guarded when they have the charge for it.
- **Joining:** units sent to guard what one of your guard groups already guards join that group.
- **Formations:** a formation sent to guard forms a Column and follows the guarded.
- **Mark:** the guarded, and the allied soldiers within 15 m of it, show a guard sign when you give the order.
- **End:** the guard ends when the guarded is destroyed.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** every unit except [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md), [Spy](../units/spy.md), [The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md), [Trebuchet](../units/trebuchet.md), [Undead Legion](../units/undead-legion.md)
- Hold **G** and right-click a unit or building (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#follow-and-guard)).

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Guard siege engines and supply wagons on the march.
- Put a Supply Wagon into the guard group: it heals the guarded on the way.
