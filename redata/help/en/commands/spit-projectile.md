# Spit Gallstone

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Spit Gallstone](game:interface/icons/SIC_Spir_Projectile.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The cheapest of the [DeathWyrm](../units/infernal-engine.md)'s three ranged attacks: a heavy gallstone that hits buildings hard and splashes the units around where it lands. Press the button and click a target, or right-click the target.

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** 60 m (sight range × 5 m), a little more from high ground. A target further away: the DeathWyrm moves closer first.
- **Damage:** 70 to the unit hit, minus its armour; at least 1. Buildings lose 60 structure per hit. Spitting from above adds 5 % per metre of height, up to double.
- **Area:** a gallstone that hits a unit on land, or the ground, hurts every unit within 8 m by a tenth of the damage, minus its armour: your own and your allies' units too.
- **Cost:** 2 mana. The DeathWyrm's mana bar holds 60 and starts empty. It fills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)). The button is greyed out while there is too little.
- **One shot per order:** the DeathWyrm spits once (its animation takes about 5 s), then waits for the next order.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll. The scatter grows with distance, up to about 8 m either way at full range, down to 2 m with [experience](../mechanics/experience.md).
- Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off. A new DeathWyrm starts out holding fire.
