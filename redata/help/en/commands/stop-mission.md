# Stop converting enemy Peasants

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Stop converting enemy Peasants](game:interface/icons/sic_Stop_Mission.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Ends a [Spy](../units/spy.md)'s [Start Mission](start-mission.md): he stops winning over enemy peasants.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select the spy and click the button. It shows only while his mission runs.
- Peasants already won over carry on to your Monastery.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Spy](../units/spy.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
