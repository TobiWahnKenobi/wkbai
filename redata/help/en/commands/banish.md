# Banish Demon

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Banish Demon](game:interface/icons/SIC_Bannish.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md) sends an enemy demon away from the battle: it vanishes and reappears at home, beside its owner's nearest [Manor](../buildings/manor.md) or other building that takes in goods (never a [Village](../buildings/village-wood.md)). It is not hurt.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy demon (the banish cursor shows only over one).
- **Target:** an enemy demonic unit, the same kinds that [Exorcise Demons](exorcism.md) hurts: [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), the [Succubus](../units/succubus.md), the [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md), the [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md), the elementals, the [DeathWyrm](../units/infernal-engine.md) and the other dark creatures.
- **Range:** the inquisitor walks to within 50 m.
- **Cost:** you need at least 20 mana to give the order. The banishing then takes 1 mana for every 16.66 hit points the target has left (60 for a demon with 1000), or all your mana if that is less.
- **Limits:** it only works if some mana is left after paying. If the target has too many hit points for the bar, the mana is spent anyway and the spell fizzles in a puff of smoke. With a full bar of 90, an inquisitor can banish a demon with up to about 1500 hit points.
- **Recharge:** mana refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about 1 a second); a full bar takes about a minute and a half.

## Tips

- Wounded demons are cheap to banish: wear a big one down first, then send it home.
- A banished demon keeps its orders and simply walks back, so use the time it is away to win the fight it left.
