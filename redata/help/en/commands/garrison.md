# Garrison

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Garrison](game:interface/icons/sic_garrison.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Sends the selected units into a building (Manor, church, fort, tower). Inside they are safe, and missile troops shoot from it.
