# Inflict Earthquake

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Inflict Earthquake](game:interface/icons/SIC_Earthquake.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

An Act of God called down by the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md): the ground heaves once around the target, battering every building and wounding every unit over a wide area. No other Act of God strikes so wide an area.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit or building. A right-click never casts it.
- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** the bishop walks to within 100 m. One second after he gets there, the earth shakes.
- **Area:** every unit and building within 50 m of the target, whoever it belongs to: your own and your allies' too.
- **Effect:** each building there loses about 200 to 420 of its structure, each unit about 30 to 120 hit points (a random amount for each one). The target itself is struck twice.
- **Duration:** a single shock.
- **Cost:** 900 piety, paid by your Cathedrals and your allies' (see [Piety](pray-for-righteousness.md#piety)). A single Cathedral can only hold that much after [Witch Hunt](../upgrades/malleus-maleficarum.md). The button stays grey until they hold that much together.
- **A look at the target:** the fog of war opens over the target for about 6 s, so you see what you hit.
- **Gives you away:** the target's owner sees your bishop for 10 s after he casts.

## Tips

- Aim at the middle of a crowded town or castle: the shock reaches every building within 50 m.
- Pull your own troops back first; the earthquake doesn't spare them.
