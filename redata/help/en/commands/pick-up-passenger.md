# Embark Next unit Selected

## Role

Tells a [Cog](../units/cog.md) to fetch a unit: select the Cog and right-click the unit. The cursor shows the boarding sign over a unit it can take.

## How it works

- **Target:** a unit of yours or of an ally that fits into what is left of the Cog's load of 40. Ships, [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md) and [The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md) can't board.
- **Effect:** the unit is told to board, and the Cog lands near it, as with [Board Vessel](board-vessel.md).

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Cog](../units/cog.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
