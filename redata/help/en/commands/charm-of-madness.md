# Charm of Madness

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Charm of Madness](game:interface/icons/SIC_Charm_of_Madness.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Cast from your [Temple of the Moon](../buildings/black-mass.md) after the [Charm of Madness](../upgrades/charm-of-madness.md) research. It doesn't touch any unit: it fills one enemy's minimap with false alarms for 40 seconds, so they can't tell where the real attacks are.

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy building or unit. Click the button, then the target. There is no range limit.
- **Effect:** every 2 s the target's owner sees five blinking dots in your colour at random places up to 200 m around the target on their minimap, and now and then hears a false alarm (a fire or a scout's warning).
- **Duration:** 40 s.
- **Cost:** 300 mana, the temple's whole bar. The temple's bar holds 300 and refills by 2 every 1.9 s (almost 5 minutes from empty to full).

## Tips

- Cast it on a building far from your real attack, just before the attack starts.
