# Resupply Ammunition

## Role

Sends a unit to get fresh supplies (for missile troops, ammunition) from a store or supply wagon.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** every unit except [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), [Carrack](../units/mangonel-boat.md), [Cog](../units/cog.md), [Corsair](../units/arrow-boat.md), [Fire Boat](../units/fire-boat.md), [Galleass](../units/bombard-boat.md), [Gold Elemental](../units/gold-elemental-large.md), [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Mounted Huntress](../units/pagan-mounted-bowoman.md), [Stone Elemental](../units/stone-elemental-large.md), [The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md), [Trebuchet](../units/trebuchet.md), [Undead Legion](../units/undead-legion.md), [Wood Elemental](../units/wood-elemental.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
