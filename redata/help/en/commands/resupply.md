# Resupply Ammunition

## Role

Despite its name, this heals: a hurt unit goes to a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) and gets its hit points back. Right-click the wagon; the cursor shows the heal sign.

## How it works

- **Target:** a Supply Wagon of yours or of an ally.
- **Range:** the unit walks to within 10 m of the wagon.
- **Healing:** every second it gets a third of your Medicine back in hit points: 5 a second, about 8 after you research [Surgery](../upgrades/surgery.md). It stops when the unit is whole.
- **Cost:** 1 food each second. With no food in your stocks, nothing happens.
- **Formations:** a formation goes to within 6 m, and all its hurt members are healed together, ten times a second, each costing food.
- **Ammunition:** this command doesn't refill arrows, bullets or javelins.
