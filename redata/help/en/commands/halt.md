# Halt

## Role

Stops the selected units where they are and cancels their orders.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** every unit except [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), [Explosive Cart](../units/explosive-cart.md), [Monk](../units/monk.md), [The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md)
- Given with the **H** key (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#halt)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
