# Halt

## Role

Stops the selected units and cancels what they were doing. Press **H**, or its button.

## How it works

- **Units:** they drop their orders, leave any attack or guard group they were part of, and stand where they are.
- **Formations:** the formation stops and forms up again in its present shape where it stands.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** every unit except [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), [Explosive Cart](../units/explosive-cart.md), [Monk](../units/monk.md), [The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md)
- Given with the **H** key (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#halt)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
