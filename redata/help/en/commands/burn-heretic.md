# Burn a Heretic

## Role

Your holy man puts an end to a heretic: a peasant possessed by a [Succubus](../units/succubus.md) (see [Possess](possess.md)) who is preaching [heresy](become-heretic.md) to your peasants. The heretic burns, and the peasants who were listening go back to work.

## How it works

- **Give it:** right-click the heretic with a [Priest](../units/priest.md) or [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md) selected. The burn cursor shows only over a peasant who is preaching.
- **Target:** any unit that is preaching heresy at that moment.
- **Effect:** the holy man walks up to the heretic and sets a fire at its feet. At once, every peasant listening to the sermon is set free: it can be selected again and goes back to normal. Two seconds after he reaches the heretic, it is gone.
- **Cost:** none.

## Tips

- Every 5 s a heretic draws in more of your peasants within 50 m, so burn it quickly: each one listening is a worker lost.
