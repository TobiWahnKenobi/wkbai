# Disembark

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Disembark](game:interface/icons/sic_unload_boat.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Sets everyone on board a [Cog](../units/cog.md) ashore.

## How it works

- **Limits:** the button shows only while the Cog carries passengers and there is land they can walk on right next to it. Bring the Cog to the shore first.
- **Effect:** all passengers get off at once, at the nearest free places around the Cog, and its load goes back to 0.

- **Offered by:** [Cog](../units/cog.md)
