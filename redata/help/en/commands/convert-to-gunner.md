# Convert to Gunner

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Convert to Gunner](game:interface/icons/sic_swap_bayonnet.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The selected [Gunners (with Bayonet)](../units/gunner-bayonet.md) take their bayonets off and become missile troops again. [Convert to Bayonet](convert-to-bayonet.md) fixes them.

## How it works

- **Effect:** they can shoot again ([Fire Bullet](fire-bullet.md)) and get Hold Fire, Fire at Will, Hold Ground and Skirmish back. A hand-to-hand blow drops back to 10 damage.
- **Light infantry:** they count as light infantry again, so enemies, and the attack cursor's colours, treat them as such.
- **Time:** the swap takes as long as removing the bayonet takes on screen, and they drop whatever they were doing.
- **Formations:** a formation of bayonet gunners stays formed while they swap. A formation with other troops in it breaks up first.
