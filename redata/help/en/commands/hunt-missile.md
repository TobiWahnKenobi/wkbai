# Hunt Missile

## Role

Lets the [Imperial Archer](../units/celestial-archer.md) shoot sheep. It is not a special arrow: right-clicking a sheep (attack cursor) gives the archer its ordinary [Fire Arrow](fire-arrow.md) order at it.

## How it works

- **Target:** sheep only. Enemy units and buildings take the normal [Fire Arrow](fire-arrow.md) order.
- **Range, rate, damage, ammunition:** as for [Fire Arrow](fire-arrow.md): 125 m, one arrow about every 6 s, 20 damage, 15 ammunition per arrow.
- **What is left:** a sheep that dies leaves a carcass holding 100 food. Wounded [Archers](../units/archer.md) can eat it to heal (see [Eat Carrion](eat-carrion.md)).
