# Wickerman Sacrifice

## Role

A peasant gives its life to speed up one of the rites: [Summon the Fomorians](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-the-formorians.md) at the Temple of the Moon, [Blessings of the Goddess](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-fertility.md) at the Sacred Grove or [Summon Abaddon](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-abaddon.md) at the Wickerman. The [Witch Hunt](../upgrades/malleus-maleficarum.md) research takes sacrifices too.

## How it works

- **Target:** your building while it performs one of these rites. Right-click it with peasants selected; the cursor changes only over a building that asks for a sacrifice.
- **Effect:** each peasant walks to a spot 8 to 12 m in front of the building and dances. Each dance cuts 10 s off the rite (a rite otherwise advances one second at a time), and the peasant goes up in flames and dies 3 s later.
- **Limits:** peasants who arrive after the rite is complete simply stop. Possessed peasants can be sacrificed too.
