# Plunder

## Role

Lets [Vandavar Barbarian](../units/vandavar.md) raiders steal resources from enemy towns, then burn what they have emptied.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select the raiders and right-click the target. The cursor shows the plunder sign.
- **Targets:** an enemy [Village](../buildings/village-wood.md) that holds resources; an enemy [Warehouse](../buildings/warehouse-stone.md), Manor, Keep, Castle or Palace; or a [Cart](../units/cart.md) whose driver has been killed.
- **Range:** within 5 m.
- **Haul:** every 5 seconds, one unit of gold, or wood when there is no gold, or food when there is neither. From a Village or a cart it comes out of what that one holds; from a Warehouse or a Manor-type building, out of its owner's stocks. It goes straight into your stocks.
- **When it's empty:** the raider sets the building on fire ([Arson Attack](arson.md)). An empty cart is just left.
