# Berserker Rage

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Berserker Rage](game:interface/icons/sic_berserker.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Succubus](../units/succubus.md) drives a few soldiers into a berserker rage for 50 seconds: they hit harder and faster but guard themselves worse, and they leave their side to attack whatever is near.

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy unit (a character). Click the button, then the target.
- **Range:** 50 m.
- **Area:** up to four units that can rage, of **any side, yours included**, within 20 m of the succubus, the ones nearest the target first. Most soldiers, militia, scouts, mercenaries, the Gibbering Horde and the Undead Legion can rage; peasants, clerics and siege engines can't.
- **Effect:** each raging unit gains a quarter more hit points, a quarter more melee damage, attacks a quarter faster and moves a little faster, but loses a quarter of its armour. It joins the map's barbarians, can't be selected and attacks the units around it.
- **Duration:** 50 s; then the unit goes back to its own side. The extra hit points stay. A unit can rage again only after it has calmed down.
- **Cost:** you need 60 mana; it costs 60 mana for each unit that rages. The succubus's mana bar holds 60 and refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about a minute from empty to full). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Limits:** casting shows her true form for 8 s.
- **Counter:** if an enemy [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) stands within 50 m of the target, the spell fizzles and the 60 mana are lost.

## Tips

- Keep your own soldiers more than 20 m away from the succubus when she casts, or they go berserk too.
