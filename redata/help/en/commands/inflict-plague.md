# Inflict Plague

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Inflict Plague](game:interface/icons/SIC_Plague.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

An Act of God called down by the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md): a sickness falls on an enemy unit and the people around it, wearing them down for 30 s. It weakens; it seldom kills.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit (the cursor changes over one once there is enough piety). A right-click never casts it.
- **Target:** an enemy unit. Buildings can't be struck.
- **Range:** the bishop walks to within 100 m. One second after he gets there, the plague breaks out.
- **Area:** the target and up to 13 other units within 20 m of it, picked at random among those with more than a quarter of their health. Anyone standing there can catch it, your own units and your allies' too.
- **Effect:** every 3 s each sick unit loses 5 to 15 hit points. A unit that drops below a quarter of its health suffers no more (it doesn't get the hit points back).
- **Duration:** 30 s (ten bouts of sickness). The victims are chosen when it breaks out; the plague doesn't spread to others later.
- **Cost:** 300 piety, paid by your Cathedrals and your allies' (see [Piety](pray-for-righteousness.md#piety)). The button stays grey until they hold that much together.
- **Gives you away:** the target's owner sees your bishop for 10 s after he casts.

## Tips

- Strike a crowd before your own troops reach it, or they may fall sick too.
- It can leave a crowd of enemy soldiers at about a quarter of their health: attack as it ends.
