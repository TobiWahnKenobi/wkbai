# Greengrow

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Greengrow](game:interface/icons/SIC_Create_Tree.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) turns enemy units into trees. You can use it once you have built [The Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md).

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy unit that has hit points. Priests, monks, bishops, the Pagan casters, carts and wagons, siege engines, ships, big demons, the Imperial War Elephant and the stone and gold elementals can't be turned; the cursor doesn't change over them.
- **Range:** 35 m.
- **Effect:** the target and up to three more of its side's units within 7 m of it (picked at random, and not checked against that list) vanish and a young pine grows in each place. The new trees belong to nobody and hold 200 wood each, so anyone can chop them.
- **Cost:** 100 mana. The arch druid's mana bar holds 200. It refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about 3 minutes from empty to full), less while his elementals live (see [Awaken Wood Elemental](awaken-wood-elemental.md)). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Limits:** the button appears once a Wickerman of yours is finished, and stays even if the Wickerman is later lost.
