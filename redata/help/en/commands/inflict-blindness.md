# Inflict Blindness

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Inflict Blindness](game:interface/icons/SIC_Blindness.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

An Act of God called down by the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md): the target and the enemy units and buildings packed around it lose their sight for 45 s.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit or building (the cursor changes over a target once there is enough piety). A right-click never casts it.
- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** the bishop walks to within 200 m, the longest reach of all the Acts of God. One second after he gets there, the blindness falls.
- **Area:** every unit and building of the target's owner within 5 m of the target. Its allies' units are spared.
- **Effect:** their sight drops to nothing, so they no longer show their owner anything around them. Red flashes play over them while it lasts.
- **Duration:** 45 s, then their sight comes back.
- **Cost:** 400 piety, paid by your Cathedrals and your allies' (see [Piety](pray-for-righteousness.md#piety)). The button stays grey until they hold that much together.
- **Gives you away:** the target's owner sees your bishop for 10 s after he casts.

## Tips

- 400 piety is the whole store of a Cathedral after the [Crusade](../upgrades/crusade.md). Holy men praying at it ([Pray for Righteousness](pray-for-righteousness.md)) fill it sooner.
- The area is small: aim at a tower or a unit in a tight crowd, not at a lone scout.
