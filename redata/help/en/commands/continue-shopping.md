# Continue trading at Shop

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Continue trading at Shop](game:interface/icons/sic_shop.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Starts trade again at a [paused](pause-shopping.md) [Shop](../buildings/shop-stone.md). Its keeper goes back to turning materials into gold ([Trade at Shop](shop.md)). The button shows only while the shop is paused.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Shop](../buildings/shop-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
