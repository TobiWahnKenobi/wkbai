# Throw Javelin

## Role

The [Javelineer](../units/javelineer.md)'s throw. Right-click an enemy with javelineers selected. They also throw at enemies they see by themselves, unless you told them to [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md).

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** 45 m (the javelineer's sight range × 5 m), a little more from high ground. If the target is further away, the javelineer walks into range first. On [Hold Ground](hold-ground.md) or in a formation it gives up instead.
- **Rate:** one javelin about every 4 s, varied at random by up to 20 %.
- **Damage:** 13, minus the target's armour on the side it strikes; at least 1 always gets through. +5 % for each metre the javelineer stands above the target, up to double. Arrow-proof targets (most siege engines and carts, boats, priests, elementals, Abaddon, Michael) take only a quarter. A building loses 0.1 per javelin.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll: the javelin hits whatever its flight meets. The scatter grows with distance, up to about 2 m either way at full range, less with [experience](../mechanics/experience.md). Without a clear line to the target the javelin is lobbed high, which scatters three times as much.
- **Ammunition:** 15 per javelin from a bar of 150 (10 throws). It refills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off.
