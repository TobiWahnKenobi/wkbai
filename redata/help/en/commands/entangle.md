# Entangle

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Entangle](game:interface/icons/sic_Entangle.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md)'s trap: roots grow out of the ground around an enemy unit and pin everyone there in place for 30 seconds, hurting them a little as they struggle.

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy unit (a character, not a building). Click the button, then the target; the cursor changes only over an enemy unit when there is enough mana.
- **Range:** 70 m. The druid walks closer if needed.
- **Area:** every enemy unit within 20 m of where the target stood. Every 3 s the spell catches anyone else who walks into that circle, **your own units too**.
- **Effect:** the units caught can't move at all (their speed drops to nothing) and lose 4 hit points at once and 4 more every 3 s. The units caught at the start also drop what they were doing and stand idle for 10 s.
- **Duration:** 30 s; then everyone caught gets their speed back.
- **Cost:** 100 mana. The arch druid's mana bar holds 200. It refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about 3 minutes from empty to full), less while his elementals live (see [Awaken Wood Elemental](awaken-wood-elemental.md)). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Counter:** if an enemy [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) stands within 50 m of the target, the spell fizzles: nothing happens and the mana is still spent.

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.

## Tips

- Cast it on the front of an enemy column, then shoot the pinned units with archers.
- Don't send your own soldiers into the roots: they get stuck as well.
