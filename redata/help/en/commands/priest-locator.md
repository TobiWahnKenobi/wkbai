# Priest Locator

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Priest Locator](game:interface/icons/SIC_Unit_Locator.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Shows you where all your [Priests](../units/priest.md) are, so you can find them on the map.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select your [Church](../buildings/church-stone.md) and click the button.
- **What happens:** a white circle shrinks onto each of your Priests on the minimap, for 2 seconds. It shows every one you own, wherever it is.
- It doesn't select them or move the camera.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Church](../buildings/church-stone.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
