# Convert to Bayonet

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Convert to Bayonet](game:interface/icons/sic_swap_bayonnet.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The selected [Gunners (with Bayonet)](../units/gunner-bayonet.md) fix their bayonets: they can no longer shoot, and fight hand to hand as heavy infantry. [Convert to Gunner](convert-to-gunner.md) takes the bayonets off again.

## How it works

- **Effect:** each hand-to-hand blow does 35 damage instead of 10. The shooting commands, Hold Ground and Skirmish are gone; Attack, Attack at Will and Stand your Ground come instead.
- **Heavy infantry:** they now count as heavy infantry, so enemies, and the attack cursor's colours, treat them as such.
- **Time:** the swap takes as long as fixing the bayonet takes on screen, and they drop whatever they were doing.
- **Formations:** a formation of these gunners stays formed while they swap. A formation with other troops in it breaks up first.
