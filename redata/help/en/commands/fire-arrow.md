# Fire Arrow

## Role

The bow troops' shot. Select [Archers](../units/archer.md), Huntresses, [Imperial Archers](../units/celestial-archer.md) or [Mounted Huntresses](../units/pagan-mounted-bowoman.md) and right-click an enemy. They also shoot at enemies they see by themselves, unless you told them to [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md).

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation. The cursor is an arrow. Once you have researched [Flaming Arrow](../upgrades/flaming-arrow.md), it shows a burning arrow over enemy buildings, boats, [Explosive Carts](../units/explosive-cart.md) and [Wood Elementals](../units/wood-elemental.md), and your archers shoot flaming arrows at them (see below).
- **Range:** the archer's sight range × 5 m: 70 m for the Archer and both Huntresses, 75 m for the Mounted Huntress, 125 m for the Imperial Archer. Standing higher adds range (about 0.6 m for each metre above the map's lowest ground). If the target is further away, the archer walks into range first. On [Hold Ground](hold-ground.md) or in a formation it gives up instead.
- **Rate:** one arrow per shooting animation. It takes about 3.2 s for the Archer, 4 s for the Huntresses and the Mounted Huntress and 6 s for the Imperial Archer, each varied at random by up to 20 %.
- **Damage:** Archer 15, Huntress of the Dawn 20, Huntress of the Sun 24, Imperial Archer 20, Mounted Huntress 18. The target's armour on the side the arrow strikes (front, flanks or rear) is taken off; at least 1 always gets through. Shooting from above adds 5 % per metre of height, up to double. A formation's attack bonus multiplies it.
- **Arrow-proof targets:** most siege engines and carts, boats, priests, elementals, Abaddon and Michael take only a quarter of an arrow's damage. A building loses only 0.1 per arrow.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll. An arrow hits whatever its flight path meets. The scatter grows with distance: up to about 3 m either way at full range for a new archer, less with [experience](../mechanics/experience.md). With a clear line to the target the arrow flies flat. Over a wall or a hill crest the archer lobs it in a high, slow arc that scatters three times as much.
- **Who gets hit:** arrows pass through your own and your allies' units, and through trees and fields. Other units, and any building in the way, stop them.
- **Ammunition:** each arrow costs 15 from the ammunition bar: 100 for the Archer (6 arrows), 150 for the others (10 arrows). With less than 15 left the archer stops shooting. The bar refills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- **Flaming arrows:** each costs 30 ammunition. A building it hits catches fire unless it is fire-proofed. A unit it hits loses the archer's damage, more if the unit burns easily, with no armour taken off.
- Giving this order also switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off.
