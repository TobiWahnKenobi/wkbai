# Fire Arrow

## Role

The bowman's shot at a target. It is the order missile troops use when you right-click an enemy.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Archer](../units/archer.md), [Celestial Archer](../units/celestial-archer.md), [Pagan Archer (Wickerman)](../units/pagan-archer-upgrade.md), [Pagan Archer](../units/pagan-archer.md), [Pagan Mounted Bowoman](../units/pagan-mounted-bowoman.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
