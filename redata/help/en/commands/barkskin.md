# Barkskin

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Barkskin](game:interface/icons/sic_Barkskin.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) turns the skin of friendly units to bark for 20 seconds, so arrows and javelins barely hurt them.

## How it works

- **Target:** one of your or an ally's units (a character). Click the button, then the unit.
- **Range:** 70 m.
- **Area:** the target and friendly units around it, within 20 m of it.
- **Effect:** arrows, ballista bolts and javelins do only a quarter of their damage to them. Bullets, cannon balls, rocks and melee blows are not reduced.
- **Duration:** 20 s.
- **Cost:** you need 150 mana to cast it; it then takes 5 mana for each unit it hardens. The arch druid's mana bar holds 200. It refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about 3 minutes from empty to full), less while his elementals live (see [Awaken Wood Elemental](awaken-wood-elemental.md)). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Limits:** a unit gets bark skin only once in its life: units that have had it, and units that already resist arrows (such as the [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) and the elementals), are passed over.

## Tips

- Cast it just before your soldiers walk into enemy archers or a tower's arrows.
