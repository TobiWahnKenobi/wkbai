# Scale Siege Tower

## Role

Sends foot soldiers up a [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md) docked at a wall and down on the far side. Right-click your open tower with the soldiers selected (the cursor shows them entering).

## How it works

- **Target:** one of your own open siege towers, docked with [Siege Tower Dock With Wall](siege-tower-dock-with-wall.md). An ally's tower can't be used.
- **Effect:** each soldier walks to the tower and climbs. It then lands on the ground about 10 m in front of the tower, beyond the wall, at the nearest free spot. There are no wall walks: the troops don't stay on top of the wall.
- **Can't be interrupted:** once a soldier is climbing, a new order waits until it is over.
- **Who:** foot soldiers such as Archers, Huntresses, Gunners, Spearmen, Pikemen, Men-at-Arms, Barbarians, Scouts and Sappers (the full list is below).
- **Afterwards:** the soldier carries on with any orders you queued after the climb.
