# Inflict Wrath of Heaven

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Inflict Wrath of Heaven](game:interface/icons/SIC_Firestorm.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

An Act of God called down by the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md): fire pours from the sky on the target for 16 s, burning the people caught under it and setting the buildings around it alight.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit or building (the cursor changes over a target once there is enough piety). A right-click never casts it.
- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation. The storm stays where the target stood.
- **Range:** the bishop walks to within 100 m. One second after he gets there, the storm breaks.
- **Units:** every 2 s, every unit within 30 m loses 8 hit points, whoever it belongs to: your own and your allies' too. A unit that stays the whole time loses 64.
- **Buildings:** every 2 s, each building within 20 m may catch fire. The chance depends on how fireproof it is: 1 in 5 each time for a house or a farm, 1 in 20 for a manor, almost none for a castle or a palace, and none at all for a Cathedral. A building already burning burns harder. A fire it starts burns on after the storm, until it burns out or peasants put it out.
- **Duration:** 16 s.
- **Cost:** 1000 piety, paid by your Cathedrals and your allies' (see [Piety](pray-for-righteousness.md#piety)). A single Cathedral can only hold that much after [Witch Hunt](../upgrades/malleus-maleficarum.md). The button stays grey until they hold that much together.
- **A look at the target:** the fog of war opens over the target for about 6 s, so you see what you hit.
- **Gives you away:** the target's owner sees your bishop for 10 s after he casts.

## Tips

- Its real harm is the fires it leaves behind: use it on a close-packed wooden town, and keep your own peasants and soldiers out of the 30 m.
