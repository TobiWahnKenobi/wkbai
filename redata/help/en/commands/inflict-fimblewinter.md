# Inflict Fimbulwinter

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Inflict Fimbulwinter](game:interface/icons/SIC_Fimblewinter.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

An Act of God called down by the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md): a freezing fog settles on a place for a minute, and everyone inside it is frozen nearly still. It does no damage, but a frozen village does no work and a frozen army stands waiting for your blows.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit or building (the cursor changes over a target once there is enough piety). A right-click never casts it.
- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation. The fog settles where the target stands.
- **Range:** the bishop walks to within 100 m. One second after he gets there, the fog falls.
- **Area:** every unit within 30 m of that spot, whoever it belongs to: your own and your allies' too.
- **Effect:** each unit in the fog drops what it is doing and stands still for 10 s, and its speed falls to 1 (a priest walks at 4). Every 5 s the fog catches anyone who has walked in, and anyone still inside is stopped again. A unit that gets out of the fog moves at its normal speed again.
- **Duration:** 60 s, then everyone left inside thaws.
- **Cost:** 300 piety, paid by your Cathedrals and your allies' (see [Piety](pray-for-righteousness.md#piety)). The button stays grey until they hold that much together.
- **A look at the target:** the fog of war opens over the target for about 6 s, so you see what you hit.
- **Gives you away:** the target's owner sees your bishop for 10 s after he casts.

## Tips

- Freeze an enemy army as it marches in, then strike it with arrows and artillery from outside the fog. Keep your own troops out of the fog, or they freeze too.
- On a busy village it stops the peasants' work for a whole minute.
