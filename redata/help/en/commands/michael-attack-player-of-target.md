# Archangel Attack Player Of Target

## Role

[The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md) fights on his own: from the moment he appears he marches against your enemies by himself. This order chooses which enemy player he goes after.

## How it works

- **Give it:** right-click any unit or building of the enemy player you want him to destroy (the attack cursor shows).
- **Effect:** he turns his campaign against that player. He goes first for its pagan places of worship and magic, the nearest first: the [Arcanum](../buildings/arcanum.md), [The Wickerman](../buildings/wickerman.md), the [Temple of the Moon](../buildings/black-mass.md), the [Sacred Grove](../buildings/temple.md), the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md) and the [Maypole](../buildings/maypole.md). Then he goes for all of that player's units and buildings, walls apart, the nearest first.
- **On the way:** enemies that come within 100 m are dealt with first. He uses his special attack whenever his charge bar is full and enemies are in sight.
- **Afterwards:** when that player has nothing left, he goes back to fighting all your enemies.
- **Limits:** right-clicking the player he is already fighting changes nothing.
