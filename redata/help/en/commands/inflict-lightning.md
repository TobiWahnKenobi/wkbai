# Inflict Lightning

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Inflict Lightning](game:interface/icons/SIC_Lightning.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

An Act of God called down by the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md): a bolt from the sky strikes one target. It kills almost any unit outright, hurts everyone standing near it, and sets a building on fire.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit or building (the cursor changes over a target once there is enough piety). A right-click never casts it.
- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** the bishop walks to within 100 m. One second after he gets there, the bolt falls.
- **On a unit:** it loses 400 hit points.
- **On a building:** it loses 350 of its structure and catches fire. The fire then burns and spreads like any other, until peasants put it out.
- **Around it:** every unit within 12 m loses up to 480 hit points, the most at the centre and less with distance (40 less for every metre), whoever it belongs to: your own and your allies' too. A struck unit takes this as well. Smaller bolts flash over a few of them.
- **Cost:** 700 piety, paid by your Cathedrals and your allies' (see [Piety](pray-for-righteousness.md#piety)). Building the statue that unlocks it also lets each Cathedral hold 1200. The button stays grey until they hold that much together.
- **A look at the target:** the fog of war opens over the target for about 6 s, so you see what you hit.
- **Gives you away:** the target's owner sees your bishop for 10 s after he casts.

**wkbai:** a struck building loses 6% of its full structure instead of 350 (a house about 23, a barracks 41) and is set on fire; the fire does the real harm. In the original game, 350 was more than a whole house. Units are struck as before. The same bolt falls in wkbai's [thunderstorms](../mechanics/seasons-weather.md#thunderstorms). A mod can change this with its own copy of wkbai's Lightning patch to the game set.
