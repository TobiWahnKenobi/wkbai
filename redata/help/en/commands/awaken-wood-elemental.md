# Awaken Wood Elemental

## Role

The arch druid wakes a wood elemental from the land. Right-click a suitable place (a wood, a rock or a gold seam) to give it. See [Wood Elemental](../units/wood-elemental.md).

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
