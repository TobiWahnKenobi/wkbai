# Awaken Wood Elemental

## Role

The [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) wakes a tree and turns it into a [Wood Elemental](../units/wood-elemental.md) that fights for you.

## How it works

- **Target:** a standing tree (pine or broadleaf). Right-click it; the cursor changes over a tree when he has enough mana.
- **Range:** he walks up to it (within 5 m).
- **Effect:** the tree shrinks away over about 15 s, and the elemental rises in its place.
- **Cost:** 75 mana. The arch druid's mana bar holds 200. It refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about 3 minutes from empty to full), less while his elementals live (see [Awaken Wood Elemental](awaken-wood-elemental.md)). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Upkeep:** while it lives, his bar refills a quarter slower (1.5 instead of 2 every 1.9 s). The full rate comes back when it dies. A stone elemental slows the refill by 0.66 and a gold one by 1, so with enough elementals his bar stops filling.
- **Limits:** if the arch druid dies, his wood elementals turn back into trees.
