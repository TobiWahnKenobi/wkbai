# Finish Wall

## Role

Sends peasants or sappers to finish an unfinished wall. Right-click a wall piece or gatehouse under construction; the cursor shows the repair cursor when it fits.

## How it works

- **Target:** an unfinished wall piece, corner or gatehouse that isn't burning.
- **The work:** the same as [Build Wall](build-wall.md): a fixed share each second, at most two builders on one piece, and from there on to the nearest unfinished piece within 50 m until none are left. Because they always take the nearest piece, they may start next to the one you clicked.
- **Cost:** nothing more; the wall was paid when you placed it.
