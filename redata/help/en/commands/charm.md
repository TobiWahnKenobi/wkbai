# Charm

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Charm](game:interface/icons/sic_charm.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Succubus](../units/succubus.md) bewitches an enemy unit, which then belongs to you for good.

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy unit (a character). Most soldiers, peasants, militia, merchants, scouts, spies, sappers, carts, wagons and siege engines can be charmed. Clerics, Holy Warriors, the Pagan casters, demons, elementals, ships and ghost soldiers can't: the spell fails on them, though the cursor still shows.
- **Range:** 50 m.
- **Effect:** 4 s after she reaches range, the target changes to your side, forgets its orders and stays yours.
- **Cost:** 45 mana, spent when she starts, whether it works or not. The succubus's mana bar holds 60 and refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about a minute from empty to full). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Limits:** casting shows her true form for 8 s (see [Disguises and spies](../mechanics/disguises-and-spies.md)). Not offered in [Valhalla mode](../mechanics/victory-conditions.md#valhalla-mode).
- **Counter:** if an enemy [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) stands within 50 m of the target, the charm fizzles.

## Offered by

- **Offered by:** [Succubus](../units/succubus.md)

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Charm the most expensive unit you can reach: a knight or a siege engine is worth far more than the mana.
