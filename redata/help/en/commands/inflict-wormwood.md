# Inflict Wormwood

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Inflict Wormwood](game:interface/icons/SIC_Wormwood.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

An Act of God called down by the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md): the burning star Wormwood falls from the sky beside the target. It smashes the buildings where it lands and can kill anyone near it.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit or building (the cursor changes over a target once there is enough piety). A right-click never casts it.
- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** the bishop walks to within 100 m and calls the star at once.
- **Where it lands:** up to 10 m from the target, in a random direction.
- **Buildings:** every building within 20 m of the impact loses 400 of its structure at the centre, down to 240 at the edge.
- **Units:** every unit within 10 m of the impact loses 280 hit points at the centre, down to 224 at 10 m. Then every unit within 30 m, those included, loses a random amount between 30 hit points and its whole health. Many die.
- **Whose units:** all of them: your own and your allies' too.
- **Cost:** 1000 piety, paid by your Cathedrals and your allies' (see [Piety](pray-for-righteousness.md#piety)). Building the statue that unlocks it also lets each Cathedral hold 1200. The button stays grey until they hold that much together.
- **A look at the target:** the fog of war opens over the target for about 6 s, so you see what you hit.
- **Gives you away:** the target's owner sees your bishop for 10 s after he casts.

## Tips

- The star may land 10 m off, so its 30 m reach is wider than it looks: keep your own army well back.
