# Finish Building

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Finish Building](game:interface/icons/sic_build_civic.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Sends builders back to work on an unfinished building site. Select them and right-click the site; the cursor turns into the repair cursor when the order fits.

## How it works

- **Target:** an unfinished foundation that isn't burning. Wall pieces and gatehouses use [Finish Wall](finish-wall.md) instead.
- **Who may work on what:** a [Peasant](../units/peasant.md) on any site except siege engines and [Explosive Mines](../buildings/explosive-mine.md) (sapper work) and the [Henge](../buildings/henge.md) (High Priestess work). A [Sapper](../units/sapper.md) only on the sapper sites.
- **The work:** the same as [Build](build.md): every stroke adds a fixed share of the building's strength, several builders add up, and nothing more is paid.
- **Limits:** a unit under [Possess](possess.md) can't take the order.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Peasant](../units/peasant.md), [Sapper](../units/sapper.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Set a building's rally point on an unfinished site: the new peasants go straight to building it.
