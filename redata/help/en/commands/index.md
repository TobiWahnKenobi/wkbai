# Commands

Every order and button that units and buildings offer. Some have a button of their own; others are given with the mouse, usually a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md)). Training a unit, putting up a building and buying research are buttons too: their pages are the [unit](../units/index.md), [building](../buildings/index.md) and [upgrade](../upgrades/index.md) pages.

![An archer selected: its command buttons round the selection panel](shot:gamedata-commands)

Spells, prayers and acts of God draw on a charge that refills over time; their buttons stay grey until there is enough.
