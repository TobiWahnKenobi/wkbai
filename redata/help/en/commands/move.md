# Move

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Move](game:interface/icons/sic_move.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Sends the selected units to a place. With a unit selected, a right-click on open ground does the same.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** every unit except [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), [Monk](../units/monk.md), [The Archangel, Sword of God](../units/michael.md), [Trebuchet](../units/trebuchet.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Hold the button and drag to set the direction the group faces when it arrives (see [orders](../controls/orders.md)).
