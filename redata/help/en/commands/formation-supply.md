# Distribute Food Rations (Formation Supply)

## Role

A [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) heals the wounded men of one of your formations with food. Right-click a unit in a formation of yours that has wounded men; the cursor shows the heal cursor.

## How it works

- **Target:** a formation of yours with at least one wounded member.
- **Reach:** the wagon drives to within 5 m of the unit you clicked.
- **Effect:** each second, every wounded member of that formation gets 5 hit points back (about 8 after [Surgery](../upgrades/surgery.md)), never above full. Machines and elementals in it don't count.
- **Cost:** 1 food from your stock for every man healed, every second. With no food left, the wagon stops healing.
- **When it ends:** when nobody in the formation is wounded any more.
