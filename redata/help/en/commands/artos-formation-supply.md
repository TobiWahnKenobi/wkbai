# Distribute Food Rations (Baron Artos Formation Supply)

## Role

The [Holy Warrior](../units/monastic-knight.md) hands out food to one of your [formations](../controls/formations.md): every wounded soldier in it gets back a few hit points for each ration, paid from your food store.

## How it works

- **Give it:** right-click a unit of one of your formations in which someone is wounded (the healing cursor shows only then).
- **Target:** your own formations only, not an ally's.
- **Range:** he comes to within 5 m of the unit you clicked and keeps handing out rations from there.
- **Effect:** each ration gives a wounded member of the formation back a third of your medicine: 5 hit points, about 8 after [Surgery](../upgrades/surgery.md), never more than it has lost. Machines in the formation are left out.
- **Cost:** 1 food for every ration handed out. It uses no mana.
- **Ends:** when nobody in the formation is wounded any more, or when your food runs out.

## Tips

- It heals the whole formation, not one soldier at a time like [Heal](monastic-knight-heal.md), and it saves his mana for healing and [Exorcise Demons](exorcism.md). Keep an eye on your food while he works.
