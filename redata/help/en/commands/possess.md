# Possess

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Possess](game:interface/icons/SIC_Possesion.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Succubus](../units/succubus.md) takes over an enemy peasant. It becomes your secret agent: it still looks like the enemy's peasant, and it can [Spread Heresy](become-heretic.md), open the way for a [Dark Portal](dark-portal.md), or be sacrificed in your rites ([Wickerman Sacrifice](wickerman-sacrifice.md)).

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy [Peasant](../units/peasant.md).
- **Range:** 50 m.
- **Effect:** 4 s after she reaches range, the peasant drops its work and becomes your possessed peasant, in the enemy's peasant clothes. Enemies see it as one of their own peasants.
- **Cost:** you need 60 mana to cast it; it spends 45. The succubus's mana bar holds 60 and refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about a minute from empty to full). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Limits:** casting shows her true form for 8 s. Not offered in [Valhalla mode](../mechanics/victory-conditions.md#valhalla-mode).
- **Counter:** if an enemy [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) stands within 50 m of the peasant, the spell fizzles. Enemy priests, bishops, inquisitors, spies and Pagan casters who see a possessed peasant unmask it; it takes on its disguise again once none of them sees it (see [Disguises and spies](../mechanics/disguises-and-spies.md)).
