# Open Stockade Gatehouse

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Open Stockade Gatehouse](game:interface/icons/sic_open_gate.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Opens the stockade gatehouse so units can pass through.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Stockade Gatehouse](../buildings/stockade-gatehouse-closed.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- An open gate lets the enemy in too: close it when he is near.
