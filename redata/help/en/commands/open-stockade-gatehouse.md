# Open (Stockade Gatehouse)

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Open (Stockade Gatehouse)](game:interface/icons/sic_open_gate.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Opens your closed [Stockade Gatehouse](../buildings/stockade-gatehouse-closed.md) so units can pass through the wall.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select the closed gatehouse and click the button.
- **What happens:** the gate plays its opening animation and sound; when that ends, the gatehouse is open and the way through is clear. Its button is now [Close](close-stockade-gatehouse.md).
- **Limits:** clicking again while it opens does nothing.
- A newly built gatehouse starts open.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Stockade Gatehouse](../buildings/stockade-gatehouse-closed.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- An open gate lets the enemy in too: close it when he is near.
