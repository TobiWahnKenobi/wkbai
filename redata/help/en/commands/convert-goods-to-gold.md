# Convert Goods to Gold

## Role

The second half of a [Merchant](../units/merchant.md)'s trade: he brings the trade goods he bought at a [Trading Post](../buildings/trading-post.md) home and turns them into gold. He does it by himself after trading; you can also right-click one of your stores while he carries goods (the cursor shows the shop cursor).

## How it works

- **Where to:** your nearest Manor-type building or [Warehouse](../buildings/warehouse-stone.md), or a [Village](../buildings/village-wood.md) of yours on the way.
- **Load:** at the Trading Post the merchant takes 1 trade good every 2 seconds, up to 10.
- **Gold:** the further the store from the Trading Post, the more he gets: the goods times the distance in metres / 400, plus the distance / 50. A full load carried 400 m gives 18 gold, 800 m 36 gold. A Trading Post of an enemy adds 50 gold to each trip.
- **At a Village** the gold goes into the village's store, for a [Cart](../units/cart.md) to take on.
