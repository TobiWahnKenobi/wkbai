# Devour

## Role

The [Gibbering Horde](../units/gibbering-horde.md) eats the land bare: trees, farms, stone and gold. As it feeds it multiplies.

## How it works

- **Target:** a tree, a farm, a rock outcrop or a gold deposit. Right-click it; the cursor changes over these.
- **Effect:** every 3 s the creature takes 1 wood, 1 point of a farm's strength, 1 stone or 1 gold from it. That feeds it 1 point for wood, 2 for a farm or stone, 3 for gold; its bar shows how full it is.
- **Splitting:** at 40 points it splits off a new Gibbering Horde creature, which goes straight for the nearest resource within 100 m. At your population limit no new one appears and the points are lost.
- **Cost:** free.
