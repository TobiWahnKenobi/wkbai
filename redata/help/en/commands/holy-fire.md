# Holy Flame

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Holy Flame](game:interface/icons/SIC_Holy_Fire.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md)'s ranged attack: he hurls balls of holy fire at an enemy, one after another, while his mana lasts.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy unit or building.
- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** the inquisitor's sight, 70 m. He needs a clear line of sight; if something blocks it, he shows that he can't see the target and doesn't throw.
- **Rate:** one fireball about every 5 s (4 to 6 s).
- **Damage:** each fireball carries the inquisitor's missile damage, 30 against units and 25 against buildings, before armour. A fireball that hits a unit also singes the units within 8 m of it, friend or foe, for a tenth of that.
- **Accuracy:** fireballs miss less at short range and as the inquisitor gains experience.
- **Cost:** 15 mana a fireball. He keeps throwing until the target is gone or his mana runs short.
- **Recharge:** mana refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about 1 a second); a full bar of 90 is six fireballs. The button stays grey below 15.
- **Hold Fire:** giving this order lifts [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md).

## Tips

- Six fireballs and then a long wait: save the mana for [Exorcise Demons](exorcism.md) or [Banish Demon](banish.md) when demons are about.
