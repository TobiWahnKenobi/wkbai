# Ghost Army

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Ghost Army](game:interface/icons/SIC_Create_Ghost_Army.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) conjures a fake army of 40 Pagan soldiers to fool the enemy. It needs the [Ghost Army](../upgrades/ghost-army.md) research. You can move the ghosts like real troops, but they never shoot, and they vanish as soon as an enemy unit walks into them.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** a button; no target. The ghosts appear 10 to 20 m in front of her, in a line formation that you can move like any other.
- **Effect:** 40 ghost soldiers, either all [Warrioress of the Dawn](../units/pagan-spearwoman.md) or all [Huntress of the Dawn](../units/pagan-archer.md) (at random). They eat no food, never shoot, and missiles pass through them without harm. They can't be charmed, enraged or turned into a horde. Only you see the shimmer that gives them away.
- **Duration:** until an enemy unit walks into any one of them; then the whole army disappears.
- **Cost:** 60 mana, the whole bar. The high priestess's mana bar holds 60 and refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about a minute from empty to full). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Limits:** one ghost army per high priestess at a time. The button comes back when her army has vanished.
