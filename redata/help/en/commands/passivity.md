# Passivity

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Passivity](game:interface/icons/SIC_Passive.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Sets the selected units to passive: they do not chase enemies on their own and only fight back when attacked.

## Tips

- Use it to keep a line in place, or to stop troops being lured away.
