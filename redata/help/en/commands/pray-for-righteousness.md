# Pray for Righteousness

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Pray for Righteousness](game:interface/icons/sic_pray.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

A holy man prays at a [Cathedral](../buildings/cathedral-stone.md) to fill its piety faster, the store that pays for the [Bishop](../units/bishop.md)'s Acts of God. At a [Statue of the Archangel](../buildings/michael-statue.md) that is making its [Sacrifice for Michael](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-michael.md), the prayers bring the Archangel sooner.

## How it works

- **Give it:** right-click a Cathedral of yours or an ally's, or a Statue of the Archangel during its sacrifice, with a [Priest](../units/priest.md), [Bishop](../units/bishop.md) or [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md) selected (the prayer cursor shows). On a building that trains holy men, the button sends each new one there (see [A building's new units](../controls/orders.md#a-buildings-new-units)).
- **Range:** he stands right beside the building.
- **Effect:** every 1.1 s he adds piety: a priest 0.3, a bishop 0.6, an inquisitor 1. At the statue the same amount goes into the sacrifice's progress.
- **Limits:** any number of holy men can pray at one building. Prayers stop counting while the bar is full; the holy man stays until you give him another order.
- **Cost:** none.

## Piety

The Acts of God (the bishop's [Visions](inflict-vision.md), [Blindness](inflict-blindness.md), [Plague](inflict-plague.md), [Fimbulwinter](inflict-fimblewinter.md), [Earthquake](inflict-earthquake.md), [Lightning](inflict-lightning.md), [Wrath of Heaven](inflict-firestorm.md) and [Wormwood](inflict-wormwood.md)) are paid from the piety of all the Cathedrals of you and your allies together. Each Cathedral pays its share of the cost, in proportion to what it holds.

- **On its own:** a Cathedral gains no piety until you have researched the [Crusade](../upgrades/crusade.md). From then on each one gains 1.4 every 1.9 s (about 44 a minute).
- **How much it holds:** 100 at first, 400 after the Crusade, 1000 after [Witch Hunt](../upgrades/malleus-maleficarum.md), 1200 once a [Statue of the Archangel](../buildings/michael-statue.md) is built.
- **Praying:** holy men add piety on top, and they can fill a Cathedral even before the Crusade, up to its first 100.

## Tips

- Three praying priests add about as much as the Cathedral gains on its own.
- Before the Crusade, prayer is the only way to fill a Cathedral, and 100 piety is exactly what [Visions](inflict-vision.md) costs.
