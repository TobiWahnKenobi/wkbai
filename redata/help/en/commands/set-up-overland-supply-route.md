# Up Overland Supply Line

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Up Overland Supply Line](game:interface/icons/SIC_Set_Up_Overland_Supply_Route.tga)

## Role

Sets up a supply route over land from this Village or Dock: carts carry goods along it on their own.
