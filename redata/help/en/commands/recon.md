# Reconnoitre

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Reconnoitre](game:interface/icons/SIC_Recon.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Sends a [Scout](../units/scout.md) off to explore the enemy's lands by himself, keeping out of harm's way.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select the scout and click the button. While he explores, the button is [Stop Recon](stop-recon.md).
- **Where he goes:** he picks an enemy Manor, Keep, Castle, Palace or [Warehouse](../buildings/warehouse-stone.md) at random and goes there. When he has reached it, he picks the next. With more than one enemy, he doesn't go to the player he looked at last.
- **Danger:** when he sees an enemy able to kill him (light infantry, light cavalry, towers, forts, gatehouses, Manors and palaces), or when he is hurt, he backs off about 100 m away from it and the enemies within 50 m of it, then carries on.
- **Healing:** below half his health, if you have a manned [Monastery](../buildings/monastery.md), he goes to heal before he carries on.
- **Limits:** not in [Valhalla mode](../mechanics/victory-conditions.md#valhalla-mode).
