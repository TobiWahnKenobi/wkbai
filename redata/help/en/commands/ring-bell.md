# Ring Bell

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Ring Bell](game:interface/icons/SIC_Bell.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Rings the town's bell: peasants nearby stop work and shelter in the building.

<!-- gen:trained -->

<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Ring it when raiders appear, then use [Recall Peasants](recall-peasants.md) to send them back once the danger has passed.
