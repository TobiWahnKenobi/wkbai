# Follow Next Unit Selected

## Role

The selected units follow a unit or formation and keep near it until you give them another order. Hold **F** and right-click the one to follow; the cursor shows the follow sign.

## How it works

- **Target:** a unit or a formation, yours or another player's.
- **Spacing:** following one of your own, each follower keeps its own spot, up to 10 m to the side and front of it, so they don't crowd onto it.
- **End:** the order ends when the followed unit is gone.
- **Who:** the [Dragoon](../units/mounted-hand-gunner.md) has the command, and every formation has it.
