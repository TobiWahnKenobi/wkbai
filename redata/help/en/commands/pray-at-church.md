# Pray

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Pray](game:interface/icons/sic_pray.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

A holy man takes charge of a [Church](../buildings/church-stone.md). Your peasants who worship there come away able to carry more, and the higher the holy man, the bigger the gain.

## How it works

- **Give it:** right-click a Church with a [Priest](../units/priest.md), [Bishop](../units/bishop.md) or [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md) selected (the prayer cursor shows over a Church that has no holy man yet). On a building that trains holy men, the button sends each new one to pray (see [A building's new units](../controls/orders.md#a-buildings-new-units)).
- **Limits:** one holy man per Church. He walks to it and stays there, praying, until you give him another order.
- **Effect:** every seventh delivery, a peasant with a Church within 50 m goes there to worship. Without a holy man it then carries 12 instead of 10. With a priest it carries 13, a bishop 14, an inquisitor 15.
- **Ends:** when the holy man leaves, the Church goes back to the plain bonus.
- **Cost:** none.

## Offered by

- **Offered by:** [Bishop](../units/bishop.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [Priest](../units/priest.md)

## Tips

- A priest who has nothing else to do earns his keep at the Church nearest your busiest fields and woods.
- Not to be confused with [Pray for Righteousness](pray-for-righteousness.md), which fills the Cathedral's piety for the Acts of God.
