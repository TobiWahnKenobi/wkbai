# Acid Spray

## Role

The [Spider Demon](../units/horde-spewer.md)'s attack: a cone of acid that burns every enemy unit and building in front of it. Right-click an enemy (slime cursor).

## How it works

- **Range:** the Spider Demon closes to 30 m of its target (sight range × 5 m).
- **Area:** each spray hits every enemy unit and building within 35 m inside a 45° arc in front of the Spider Demon, not only the target. Your own and allied units are safe.
- **Damage:** 30 hit points and 50 structure to each, with no armour taken off.
- **Rate:** one spray about every 4 s, varied at random by up to 20 %.
- **Ammunition:** 25 per spray from a bar of 150 (6 sprays). It refills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off.
