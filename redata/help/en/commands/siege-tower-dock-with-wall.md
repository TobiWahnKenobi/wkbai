# Siege Tower Dock With Wall

## Role

Pushes the siege tower up to an enemy wall and lowers its ramp. Give it with a right-click on the wall.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
