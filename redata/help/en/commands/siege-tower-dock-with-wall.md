# Siege Tower Dock With Wall

## Role

Pushes a [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md) up against a wall and lowers its ramp, so your foot soldiers can climb over with [Scale Siege Tower](scale-siege-tower.md). Right-click a wall section with the tower selected (the cursor shows a tower against a wall).

## How it works

- **Target:** a section of [Stockade](../buildings/stockade.md), [Fortified Wall](../buildings/unaligned-wall.md), [Battlements](../buildings/technological-wall.md) or [Ramparts](../buildings/celestial-wall.md). Gatehouses and towers don't count.
- **One tower per section:** the cursor only offers sections with no tower against them. If the section is taken, or another tower is already heading there, the tower goes to the nearest free section within 25 m of the one you clicked.
- **Effect:** the tower drives right up to the wall, lowers its ramp and turns to face the wall. It is then an open tower, which can't move until you press [Close Siege Tower](close-siege-tower.md).
