# Deliver Resources (Cog Set Up Drop Off Dock)

## Role

Puts a [Cog](../units/cog.md) to work carrying goods overseas from a [Dock](../buildings/dock-wood.md). Select the Cog and right-click the Dock to load at; the cursor shows the delivery sign.

## How it works

- **Target:** a Dock of yours or of an ally.
- **Loading:** the Cog waits at the Dock until at least 50 goods are stored there and no cart or other Cog is loading, checking every 5 s. Then it takes up to 500 food, wood and gold in one load.
- **Delivery:** it sails to the Dock chosen with [Up Overseas Supply Line](set-up-oversea-supply-route.md), or, when none was chosen, to your Dock nearest to a Manor-type building that a cart can reach. It unloads there in 3 s.
- **Again:** then it goes back for the next load, until you give it another order.
