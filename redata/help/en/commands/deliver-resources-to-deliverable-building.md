# Deliver Resources (to Deliverable Building)

## Role

Carries what a unit holds to a building that takes goods. Gatherers do it by themselves when their load is full; you can also right-click one of your stores while the unit carries something (the cursor shows the delivery cursor).

## How it works

- **Where to:** your [Manor](../buildings/manor.md) (or Keep, Castle, Palace), a [Warehouse](../buildings/warehouse-stone.md) or a [Village](../buildings/village-wood.md). The unit always heads for the nearest of these, whichever you clicked.
- **Into the stock:** at a Manor-type building or a Warehouse the goods go straight into your stock. At a Village they stay in the village's own store until a [Cart](../units/cart.md) takes them on ([Transport Resources](transport-resources-from-village-to-global-stocks.md)).
- **What arrives:** wood and gold as they are; each stone as 2 materials; food less the share your army eats ([the upkeep penalty](../mechanics/population-and-food.md#the-upkeep-penalty)). When your army eats more than your farmers support, a sign appears over the delivering peasant.
- **Unloading:** the unit puts everything down at once. A gatherer then goes back to his work; given on its own, the order ends there.
- **Day of rest:** each delivery counts towards a peasant's seventh trip ([church and maypole](../mechanics/peasants-and-work.md#church-and-maypole)).
