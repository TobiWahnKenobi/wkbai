# Deliver Resources (to Deliverable Building)

## Role

Carries goods to a building that takes them (a store, Village or Manor). Workers do it by themselves; right-click a store to send them to that one.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Cart](../units/cart.md), [Merchant](../units/merchant.md), [Peasant](../units/peasant.md), [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
