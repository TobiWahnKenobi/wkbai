# Clear Supply Line

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Clear Supply Line](game:interface/icons/SIC_Clear_Supply_Route.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Removes the supply lines set up from this [Village](../buildings/village-wood.md) or [Dock](../buildings/dock-wood.md), both the [overland](set-up-overland-supply-route.md) and the [overseas](set-up-oversea-supply-route.md) one. Its carts then take their goods to the nearest store again.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Dock](../buildings/dock-wood.md), [Village](../buildings/village-wood.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
