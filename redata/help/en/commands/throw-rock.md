# Throw Rock

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Throw Rock](game:interface/icons/SIC_Missile_Attack.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [Fomorian Beast](../units/rock-thrower.md) hurls a boulder that works like a trebuchet stone: it smashes buildings and hurts units near where it lands. Press the button and click a target, or right-click it.

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation. The cursor shows "too close" under 15 m and the order is refused.
- **Range:** 15 m to about 95 m (sight range × 7.9 m), a little more from high ground. A target further away: the beast walks into range first.
- **Rate:** one rock about every 3 s, varied at random by up to 20 %.
- **Damage:** 35 to the unit hit, minus its armour; at least 1. Buildings lose 50 structure per hit. Throwing from above adds 5 % per metre of height, up to double.
- **Area:** a rock that hits a unit on land, or the ground, hurts every unit within 8 m by a tenth of the damage, minus its armour: your own and your allies' units too. One that hits a building hurts the units building or repairing it the same way.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll. The scatter grows with distance, up to about 8 m either way at full range, down to 2 m with [experience](../mechanics/experience.md).
- **Ammunition:** 30 per rock from a bar of 100 (3 rocks). It refills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- **Hold fire:** a new Fomorian Beast starts out holding fire: it only throws when you order it, until you press [Fire at Will](fire-at-will.md). Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off. Once it is attacking a target you gave it, Hold Fire doesn't stop it.
