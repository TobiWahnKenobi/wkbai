# Spy on Infantry

## Role

A [Spy](../units/spy.md) studies an enemy [Barracks](../buildings/barracks-stone.md) or [Hall of Warriors](../buildings/hall-of-warriors.md) and then shows you all that player's foot soldiers.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select the spy and right-click an enemy Barracks or Hall of Warriors. The cursor shows the spy sign over them.
- **Range and time:** he goes within 5 m of the building and watches it for 5 seconds; a bar counts down.
- **Effect:** every heavy and light infantry unit of the building's owner, wherever it is on the map, is shown to you through the fog of war for 10 seconds (see [Fog of war](../mechanics/fog-of-war.md#brief-reveals)).
- **Cost:** nothing. The spy is free again afterwards.
- **Cavalry:** right-clicking an enemy [Stables](../buildings/stables.md) does the same for his cavalry (Spy on Cavalry).
