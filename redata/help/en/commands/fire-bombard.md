# Fire (Bombard)

## Role

The cannon shot of the [Bombard](../units/bombard.md) and the [Galleass](../units/bombard-boat.md): an iron ball against buildings and massed troops. Right-click an enemy unit or building.

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation. For the Bombard, the cursor shows "too close" under 20 m and the order is refused.
- **Range:** 150 m for both (sight range × 12.5 m for the Bombard, × 7.5 m for the Galleass), a little more from high ground; the Bombard needs at least 20 m. A target further away: the cannon moves into range first.
- **Rate:** one shot about every 10 s, varied at random by up to 20 %.
- **Damage:** Bombard 45, Galleass 50 to the unit hit, minus its armour; at least 1. Buildings lose 50 (Galleass 35) structure per hit. Shooting from above adds 5 % per metre of height, up to double.
- **Area:** a ball that hits a unit on land, or the ground, hurts every unit within 8 m by a tenth of the damage, minus its armour: your own and your allies' units too. One that hits a building hurts the units building or repairing it the same way.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll. The scatter grows with distance, up to about 8 m either way at full range, down to 1 m with [experience](../mechanics/experience.md).
- **Ammunition:** the Bombard uses 45 per shot from a bar of 150 (3 shots). It refills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)). The Galleass's shots cost nothing.
- **Hold fire:** a new Bombard starts out holding fire: it only shoots when you order it, until you press [Fire at Will](fire-at-will.md). Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off; pressing Hold Fire later ends the attack.
