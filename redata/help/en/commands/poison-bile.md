# Squirt Poison Bile

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Squirt Poison Bile](game:interface/icons/SIC_Poison_Bile.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

One of the [DeathWyrm](../units/infernal-engine.md)'s three ranged attacks: a ball of bile that leaves a poison cloud, wearing down every unit around where it lands. Press the button and click a target, or right-click the target.

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation.
- **Range:** 60 m (sight range × 5 m), a little more from high ground. A target further away: the DeathWyrm moves closer first.
- **Effect:** where the bile lands, every unit within 15 m loses 10 hit points every 3 s. Armour doesn't help, and your own and allied units are hurt too.
- **Duration:** 8 ticks, about 24 s: up to 80 hit points for a unit that stays in the cloud.
- **Cost:** 30 mana. The DeathWyrm's mana bar holds 60 and starts empty. It fills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)). The button is greyed out while there is too little.
- **One shot per order:** the DeathWyrm spits once, then waits for the next order.
- Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off.
