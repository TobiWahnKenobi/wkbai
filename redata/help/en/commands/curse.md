# Curse

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Curse](game:interface/icons/sic_Curse.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The Pagan casters' answer to an enemy army: a curse that halves the damage of the target and the units around it for 30 seconds. The [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) and the [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) both know it.

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy unit (a character).
- **Range:** 70 m for the arch druid, 50 m for the high priestess.
- **Area:** the target and up to 7 more of its side's units within 10 m of it.
- **Effect:** their melee damage, missile damage and damage to buildings are halved. A unit that was blessed loses the blessing first (see [Bless](bless.md)).
- **Duration:** 30 s.
- **Cost:** 45 mana. The high priestess's mana bar holds 60, the arch druid's 200; both refill by 2 every 1.9 s (about a minute and about 3 minutes from empty to full), the druid's less while his elementals live. Supply wagons don't refill them.
- **Limits:** a unit already cursed isn't cursed again.
- **Counter:** if an enemy [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) stands within 50 m of the target, the curse fizzles and the 45 mana are lost.
