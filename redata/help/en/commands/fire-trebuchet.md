# Fire Trebuchet

## Role

The trebuchet's throw, given with a right-click on a target in range. The trebuchet must be set up, not packed for travel.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Trebuchet](../units/trebuchet.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
