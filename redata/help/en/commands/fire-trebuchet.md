# Fire (Trebuchet)

## Role

The [Trebuchet](../units/trebuchet.md)'s throw: the longest-reaching and hardest-hitting stone in the game, made for knocking down walls and buildings. Right-click a target in range. The trebuchet must be set up: packed for travel it can't fire, and set up it can't move.

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation. The cursor shows "too close" under 20 m and a crossed-out arrow beyond range; in both cases nothing happens.
- **Range:** 20 m to 160 m (sight range × 13.3 m), a little more from high ground.
- **Rate:** one throw about every 12 s, varied at random by up to 20 %.
- **Damage:** 75 to the unit hit, minus its armour; at least 1. Buildings lose 65 structure per hit. Shooting from above adds 5 % per metre of height, up to double.
- **Area:** a ball that hits a unit on land, or the ground, hurts every unit within 8 m by a tenth of the damage, minus its armour: your own and your allies' units too. One that hits a building hurts the units building or repairing it the same way. A ball landing on a field damages the field.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll. The scatter grows with distance, up to about 8 m either way at full range, down to 2 m with [experience](../mechanics/experience.md). A shot at a boat isn't scattered.
- **Ammunition:** 35 per throw from a bar of 150 (4 throws). It refills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s, and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- **With [Greek Fire](../upgrades/greek-fire.md):** every throw is Greek fire and costs 75. The ball does its normal damage to what it hits (no 8 m blast). It sets every building within 10 m on fire and bursts into 7 burning pieces that land 10 to 20 m around. Each piece sets what it hits on fire and takes 10 to 40 hit points and 15 structure from it. A piece that lands on the ground burns there for 10 s and sets a field under it alight.
- **With [Explosives](../upgrades/grapeshot.md) (and no Greek fire):** every throw is a shell of shot and costs 75. Besides the hit itself, every unit and building within 30 m, yours included, loses 10 to 40 hit points and 15 structure, with no armour taken off. It scatters twice as much as a stone.
- **Hold fire:** a new Trebuchet starts out holding fire: it only shoots when you order it, until you press [Fire at Will](fire-at-will.md). Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off.
