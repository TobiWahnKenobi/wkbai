# Arson Attack

## Role

Sets an enemy building on fire. [Spies](../units/spy.md), [Mercenaries](../units/mercenary.md), [Barbarians](../units/barbarian.md) and [Vandavar Barbarians](../units/vandavar.md) can do it.

## How it works

- **How to give it:** select the units and right-click an enemy building that is still standing. The cursor shows the arson sign.
- **Formations:** a formation breaks up, and every member goes to burn the target.
- **Time:** the unit goes right up to the building and works on it for 15 seconds; a bar counts down.
- **Effect:** a building not yet on fire catches fire and loses 5 strength at once. Its owner gets an orange circle on his minimap and a warning sound. A building already burning burns harder, up to the fire's maximum. The fire then keeps damaging it (see [Fire](../mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#fire)).
- **Spies:** for 8 seconds after a spy sets a fire, computer players' defences can notice him, disguise or not.
- **Counter:** peasants on [Extinguish Fire](extinguish.md) put fires out.
