# Arson

## Role

Sets an enemy building on fire.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [Barbarian](../units/barbarian.md), [Mercenary](../units/mercenary.md), [Spy](../units/spy.md), [Vandavar](../units/vandavar.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->
