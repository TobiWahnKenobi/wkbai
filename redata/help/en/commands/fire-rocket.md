# Fire Rocket

## Role

The [Rocket Launcher](../units/rocket-launcher.md)'s volley: one huge blast that hurts everything around the impact, friend or foe. Right-click a target. The launcher must be set up ([Unlimber](unlimber.md)); packed up it can't fire.

## How it works

- **Target:** any enemy unit, building or formation, 25 m to 200 m away (sight range × 12.5 m). The cursor shows "too close" or a crossed-out arrow outside that band, and the order is then not given at all.
- **One volley per order:** the launcher fires once, then waits. Right-click again for the next one.
- **Damage:** armour doesn't help. Every unit and building within 10 m of the impact loses 150 hit points and 250 structure; everything within 30 m loses 60 and 80 more (so 210 and 330 close in). The unit or building hit directly takes another 150 and 250. This is the same for a rocket that lands on the ground. Your own and your allies' units and buildings are hurt too.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll, and rockets scatter a lot: up to about 15 m either way at full range for a new crew, down to 4 m with [experience](../mechanics/experience.md).
- **Ammunition:** a volley uses the whole bar of 40. It refills by itself, 2 points about every 2 s (about 40 s for a full bar), and by 10 every 5 s within 25 m of a [Supply Wagon](../units/supply-wagon.md) or a barracks, armoury, fort, Watch Tower, keep, castle or palace (50 m with [Logistics](../upgrades/logistics.md)).
- **Hold fire:** a new launcher starts out holding fire: it only shoots when you order it, until you press [Fire at Will](fire-at-will.md). Giving this order switches [Hold Fire](hold-fire.md) off.
