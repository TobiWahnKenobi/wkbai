# Polymorph to Gibbering Horde

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Polymorph to Gibbering Horde](game:interface/icons/sic_SummonHord.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) turns an enemy unit into a pack of [Gibbering Horde](../units/gibbering-horde.md) creatures that fight for you.

## How it works

- **Target:** an enemy unit that can be turned: peasants, militia, merchants, monks, spies, scouts, sappers, mercenaries, barbarians, and the lighter soldiers of the three paths (such as the Spearman, Archer, Knight or Warrioress of the Dawn). The cursor changes only over these.
- **Range:** 60 m.
- **Effect:** the target is held still for a moment, then dies, and one Gibbering Horde creature appears for every 10 hit points it had left (8 from a healthy peasant), one every half second, around the spot.
- **Cost:** 60 mana, the whole bar. The high priestess's mana bar holds 60 and refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about a minute from empty to full). Supply wagons don't refill it.
- **Limits:** you need room for more than 2 units under your population limit (see [the population limit](../mechanics/population-and-food.md#the-population-limit)); without it nothing happens. The creatures count towards your population.
- **Counter:** if an enemy [Monk](../units/monk.md), [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md), [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) or [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) stands within 50 m of the target, the spell fizzles and the 60 mana are lost.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Pick a healthy target: the more hit points it has, the bigger the horde.
