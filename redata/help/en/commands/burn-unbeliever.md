# Burn Unbeliever

## Role

The [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md) and the [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md) burn a preaching heretic (see [Spread Heresy](become-heretic.md)) and set its listeners free. It is the Pagan version of [Burn a Heretic](burn-heretic.md).

## How it works

- **Target:** a heretic that is preaching. Right-click it; the cursor changes over any preaching heretic, your own included.
- **Range:** the caster walks up to the heretic (within 2 m).
- **Effect:** fire bursts on the heretic, its listeners are released, and 2 s later the heretic is gone.
- **Cost:** free: it uses no mana.
