# Close Siege Tower

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Close Siege Tower](game:interface/icons/SIC_Close_Siege_Tower.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

Raises the ramp of a [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md) docked at a wall, so the tower can move again.

## How it works

- **How:** press the button on the open tower. It is the open tower's only command: docked, it can't move.
- **Effect:** the tower plays its closing animation, then is a normal siege tower again, with Move and [Siege Tower Dock With Wall](siege-tower-dock-with-wall.md).
- **Limits:** troops can no longer climb over with [Scale Siege Tower](scale-siege-tower.md) once it is closed.

## Offered by

- **Offered by:** [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md)
