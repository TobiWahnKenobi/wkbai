# Exorcise Demons

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Exorcise Demons](game:interface/icons/SIC_Exorsist.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

A holy man's strike against the creatures of darkness: it hurts an enemy demon and the demons standing beside it. Ordinary soldiers are not touched.

## How it works

- **Give it:** press the button, then click an enemy demon (the exorcism cursor shows only over one).
- **Target:** an enemy demonic unit: [Abaddon](../units/abaddon.md), the [Succubus](../units/succubus.md), the [High Priestess](../units/high-priestess.md), the [Arch Druid](../units/arch-druid.md), the [Gibbering Horde](../units/gibbering-horde.md), the [Spider Demon](../units/horde-spewer.md), the [Fomorian Beast](../units/rock-thrower.md), the [DeathWyrm](../units/infernal-engine.md), the [Behemoth](../units/behemoth.md) and the [Stone](../units/stone-elemental-large.md), [Gold](../units/gold-elemental-large.md) and [Wood](../units/wood-elemental.md) Elementals.
- **Range:** the holy man walks to within 70 m, then prays for about 3 s; the blow lands when the prayer ends.
- **Damage:** a [Priest](../units/priest.md) deals 100, an [Inquisitor](../units/inquisitor.md) 200, a [Holy Warrior](../units/monastic-knight.md) 20. Armour doesn't help.
- **Area:** the damage is shared between the target and every demon of the same owner within 10 m of it. The target takes two shares, each other demon one. A lone demon takes the whole blow.
- **Cost:** 15 mana, paid as soon as the order starts, even if the prayer is then broken off.
- **Recharge:** mana refills by 2 every 1.9 s (about 1 a second). A priest holds 60, an inquisitor 90, a holy warrior 30. The button stays grey below 15.

## Offered by

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.

## Tips

- The blow is split, so it hits hardest on a demon standing alone. Against a pack, aim at the one you most want dead: it takes a double share.
- An inquisitor with a full bar can exorcise six times in a row.
