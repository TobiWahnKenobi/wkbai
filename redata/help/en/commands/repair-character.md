# Repair (Character)

<!-- gen:facts -->
<!-- facts:static -->
| | |
|---|---|
| Cost | 1 wood |
<!-- /facts:static -->
<!-- /gen:facts -->

## Role

Mends a damaged machine or ship with timber: carts, siege engines, artillery and boats can't be healed, so your workers repair them instead.

## How it works

- **Give it:** right-click the damaged machine with a [Peasant](../units/peasant.md), [Sapper](../units/sapper.md) or [King Artos](../units/hero-technological.md) selected (the repair cursor shows only over one that needs it).
- **Target:** one of your own or an allied machine that has lost hit points: the [Cart](../units/cart.md), [Battering Ram](../units/battering-ram.md), [Siege Tower](../units/siege-tower.md), [Mangonel](../units/mangonel.md), [Trebuchet](../units/trebuchet.md), [Bombard](../units/bombard.md), [Rocket Launcher](../units/rocket-launcher.md), and the ships: [Cog](../units/cog.md), [Corsair](../units/arrow-boat.md), [Fire Boat](../units/fire-boat.md), [Carrack](../units/mangonel-boat.md) and [Galleass](../units/bombard-boat.md).
- **Range:** the worker comes to within 5 m and hammers away.
- **Effect:** each stroke of the hammer gives back 3 hit points and costs 1 wood.
- **Ends:** when the machine is whole, or when your wood runs out.

## Offered by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Offered by:** [King Artos](../units/hero-technological.md), [Peasant](../units/peasant.md), [Sapper](../units/sapper.md)
- No button of its own: you give it with a right-click on the target (see [Orders](../controls/orders.md#the-right-click)).
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- Nothing: it is there whenever the unit or building is.
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Tips

- Send several workers to a badly damaged ram or ship: each one repairs at the same time and pays its own wood.
