# Flee

<!-- gen:icon -->
![Flee](game:interface/icons/sic_run_away.tga)
<!-- /gen:icon -->

## Role

The selected units break off and run back towards your base.

## How it works

- **Where to:** towards your nearest Manor, keep, castle, palace or [Warehouse](../buildings/warehouse-stone.md) they can reach. Within 100 m of it they stop 20 m short of it; farther away they run to a point 100 m out from it, on their side.
- **Speed:** they run 10% faster than their normal pace while they flee.
- **Orders:** fleeing cancels what they were doing, and they leave any attack group.
- **Formations:** the formation breaks up, and every member flees on its own.
- **Recharge:** the button is gone for 10 s after use. Experienced units get it back sooner: 3 s sooner per point of experience, so about 6.5 s for the most experienced ([Experience](../mechanics/experience.md)).
