# Interrogate

## Role

Opens a supply crate. Right-click the crate; the cursor shows the talk sign over something that can be opened or spoken to.

## How it works

- **Target:** anything marked as ready to talk. In the game's own rules that is the supply crate; a campaign or custom map may mark other things.
- **Range:** the unit walks to within 12 m.
- **Crates:** in Valhalla games and skirmish campaign levels, a crate sits at a random place, and every 300 s it is replaced by a new one somewhere else. The first unit to reach it opens it; it can't be opened twice.
- **What's inside**, by chance:
  - **24 times in 25**, something joins your side: a siege engine (1 in 5: a [Mangonel](../units/mangonel.md) most often, else a [Bombard](../units/bombard.md) or a [Rocket Launcher](../units/rocket-launcher.md)), a monster (1 in 5: [Imperial War Elephant](../units/war-elephant.md), [Fomorian Beast](../units/rock-thrower.md), [Behemoth](../units/behemoth.md), [Spider Demon](../units/horde-spewer.md) or [DeathWyrm](../units/infernal-engine.md), the Spider Demon twice as likely), or 6 to 12 soldiers of one kind (3 in 5: [Warriors of the Sun](../units/pagan-pikeman.md), [Huntresses of the Sun](../units/pagan-archer-upgrade.md), [Imperial Pikemen](../units/man-at-arms.md), [Imperial Archers](../units/celestial-archer.md), [Gunners](../units/gunner.md), [War Riders](../units/heavy-horseman-upgrade.md) or [Imperial Knights](../units/celestial-knight.md)).
  - **1 time in 25**, a trap: the crate blows up in fire and hurts your units within 10 m, or the five units nearest to it, of any side, turn into chickens or sheep.
