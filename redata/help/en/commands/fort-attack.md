# Attack (Fort)

## Role

How towers, forts, the Manor and keeps, castles and palaces shoot. They fire on their own at enemy units in range. Right-click an enemy with the building selected (attack cursor) to make every shot go at that one target.

## How it works

- **Who shoots:** the archers inside. Each [garrisoned](garrison.md) light infantry unit fires one arrow, each garrisoned gunner one bullet. The Manor, keeps, castles and palaces also have 4 archers of their own, so they shoot even when empty; towers and forts shoot only with a garrison. With [Crossbow](../upgrades/crossbow.md) researched, every garrisoned unit fires an arrow.
- **Rate:** one round from everyone inside every 5 s. Each shot leaves from one of the 3 arrow slits nearest the target, after a random delay of up to 2 s.
- **Range:** the building's sight range × 5 m: Manor, keeps, castles and palaces 50 m, Watch Tower 75 m, Fort 90 m, stone towers 95 m, Holy Fortress 115 m. Height adds a little.
- **Targets by itself:** the nearest enemy units in range that it can see, one target for every two shooters, each shot going at the one fewest shots are already aimed at. It doesn't pick buildings.
- **Your target:** any enemy unit or building in range. All shots go at it as long as it stays an enemy within range; then the building goes back to choosing.
- **Damage:** arrows Watch Tower 18, Holy Fortress 20, the others 15; bullets Watch Tower 28, Fort 25, Holy Fortress 30. The target's armour on the side hit is taken off, and arrow-proof targets take a quarter of an arrow. Shots from a building never harm buildings.
- **Aim:** there is no hit roll. The scatter grows with distance: up to 4.5 m either way at full range for arrows, 5 m for bullets. Without a clear line from the slit the arrow is lobbed.
- **Ammunition:** none: buildings never run out.
- **Hold fire:** while a building [holds fire](hold-fire.md) it doesn't choose targets; a target you right-click is still shot (the right-click switches Hold Fire off).
