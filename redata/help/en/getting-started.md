# Your first skirmish

A skirmish is a single game against the computer on a map of your choice. This page walks through the first ten minutes.

![A skirmish just started: your manor, its palisade and four peasants](shot:core-game-start)

## Start a game

1. In the [main menu](ui/frontend/main.md) choose **Skirmish**.
2. On the [Players tab](ui/frontend/skirmish/players.md) pick a map; *2 Player 4 Peaks* is a good first one. Put an AI General in the second position.
3. Leave the [Settings](ui/frontend/skirmish/settings.md) as they are and press **Start**.

## What you see

- **The top bar** shows your population and your food, wood and gold ([top bar](ui/hud/top-bar.md)).
- **The lower left** is the [selection panel](ui/hud/selection-panel.md): what you have selected, and the [command buttons](ui/hud/command-panel.md) round its rim.
- **The lower right** is the [minimap](ui/hud/minimap.md).
- In the middle is your [Manor](buildings/manor.md) inside its palisade, with a few [peasants](units/peasant.md) in front of it.

## The first minutes

1. **Put the peasants to work.** Select a peasant with a left click, then right-click a tree for wood, or a field for food. See [peasants and work](mechanics/peasants-and-work.md).
2. **Train more peasants.** Select the manor and press its peasant button a few times. More hands are the fastest way to grow ([resources](mechanics/resources.md)).
3. **Build farms** for food: select a peasant, open the Economic tab of the build menu and place a farm on flat land near the manor ([placing buildings](controls/building-placement.md)).
4. **Build a barracks** once wood comes in, and train a few [spearmen](units/spearman.md) and [archers](units/archer.md) to guard your town.
5. **Look around.** Drag the minimap or move the mouse to the screen's edge to move the [camera](controls/camera.md); the dark parts of the map are under the [fog of war](mechanics/fog-of-war.md).

## Winning

You win when no enemy can fight on; the [victory conditions](mechanics/victory-conditions.md) page explains the details and the other game modes. Press **Esc** at any time for the [game menu](ui/frontend/ingame-menu.md): save, options or leave.
