## Role

The Renaissance Keep is your Manor's second level. The Manor becomes this Keep by itself when you finish a Windmill or Sawmill. It is stronger (structural integrity 1500), still trains peasants and carts, and opens the second tier of research at the Blacksmith.

## Built by
