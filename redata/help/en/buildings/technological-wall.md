## Role

The Renaissance wall comes after [Fortified Wall to Battlements](../upgrades/unaligned-wall-to-technological-wall.md): strength 800 per section; existing stone walls are rebuilt in this form.

## What it does

- **Blocks the way:** enemies must break through or come in by a gate.
- **Towers:** select a section and use [Upgrade to Battlement Tower](../upgrades/upgrade-to-technological-tower.md) to raise it into a tower for five archers.
- **Next:** [Battlements to Ramparts](../upgrades/technological-wall-to-celestial-wall.md) rebuilds it as Ramparts.

## Defence

- **Strength:** 800.
- **What hurts it:** soldiers barely scratch it (0.1 a blow). Siege engines, sappers and fire do the work ([siege](../mechanics/siege.md#structural-damage)).
- **Fire:** fireproof. Flaming arrows don't set it alight; a spy's arson, Greek fire and lightning still can.
- **Repair:** peasants [repair](../commands/repair.md) it, paying as they go ([strength and repair](../mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#strength-damage-and-repair)).
