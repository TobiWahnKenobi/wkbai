# Buildings

Every building you can put up, grouped by purpose. Each page has its cost and strength, who builds it, what it needs and what it unlocks.

![A grown town: Manor, Village, farms, Barracks, Stables, Guildhall and a stockade](shot:gamedata-town)

**How the town grows.** Your [Manor](manor.md) grows by itself as you build the buildings of a path: it becomes a Keep, then a Castle, then a Palace. Which buildings do it depends on the path: the Sacred Grove, Temple of the Moon and Arcanum for the Pagans; the Monastery, Cathedral and Scriptorium for the Imperial side; the Windmill or Sawmill, Observatory and University for the Renaissance. Many researches at the [Blacksmith](blacksmith.md) wait for the Keep or the Castle.

**Strength** is the building's structural integrity: how much damage it takes before it falls.
