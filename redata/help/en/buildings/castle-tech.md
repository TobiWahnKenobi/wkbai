## Role

The Renaissance Castle is your Manor's third level. The Keep becomes this Castle by itself when you finish a Observatory. It has structural integrity 2000 and opens the third tier of research at the Blacksmith; with a Castle you may also build another Manor.

## Tips

- Garrison troops inside to make it shoot harder, and use [Sanctuary (Ring Bell)](../commands/ring-bell.md) to send peasants into shelter during a raid.
- A second [Manor](manor.md) elsewhere gives you another place to train peasants and store goods.
