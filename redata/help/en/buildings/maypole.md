## Role

The maypole is the first Pagan building. It unlocks barbarians and the Temple. Building it sets you on the Pagan path: Imperial buildings and units are no longer offered.

## Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Built by:** [Peasant](../units/peasant.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Village](village-wood.md)
- Pagan or neutral side only: the button goes once you build a [Church](church-stone.md) (the Imperial path).
<!-- /gen:needs -->

## Leads to

<!-- gen:leads -->
- **Building it unlocks:** [Barbarian](../units/barbarian.md), [Temple](temple.md)
<!-- /gen:leads -->

## Commands

<!-- gen:commands -->
- None of its own.
<!-- /gen:commands -->

## Tips

- Think before you build it: it closes the Imperial path for the rest of the game.
