## Role

The maypole is the first Pagan building. It unlocks barbarians and the Sacred Grove. Building it sets you on the Pagan path: Imperial buildings and units are no longer offered.

## What it does

- **Dancing.** On every seventh delivery a peasant rests; on the way back he visits a maypole of yours or an ally's within 50 m, dances, and from then on farms at 1.5 times the rate ([church and maypole](../mechanics/peasants-and-work.md#church-and-maypole)). Selecting it shows the 50 m.
- **Unlocks** barbarians and the Sacred Grove.

## Defence

- **Strength:** 200.
- **What hurts it:** soldiers barely scratch it (0.1 a blow). Siege engines, sappers and fire do the work ([siege](../mechanics/siege.md#structural-damage)).
- **Fire:** each flaming arrow that hits it has a 15% chance to set it alight. Left alone, a fire grows until the building burns down, and it can leap to buildings within 25 m. Send peasants to [Extinguish Fire](../commands/extinguish.md).
- **Repair:** peasants [repair](../commands/repair.md) it, paying as they go ([strength and repair](../mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#strength-damage-and-repair)).

- Put it among your farms: the dance helps farmers most.
