## Role

The monastery trains monks, researches [Chivalry](../upgrades/chivalry.md) (pikemen and knights) and can be raised into a Holy Chapter. It turns your Manor into the Imperial Keep.

## Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->

<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Church](church-stone.md)
- Imperial or neutral side only: the button goes once you build a [Maypole](maypole.md) (the Pagan path).
