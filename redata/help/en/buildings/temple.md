## Role

The temple trains high priestesses and holds the Pagan festivals ([Beltane](../upgrades/festival-of-beltane.md), [Samhain](../upgrades/festival-of-samhain.md)) and the [Blessings of the Goddess](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-fertility.md). It turns your Manor into the Pagan Keep and unlocks heavy horsemen, mounted bowwomen and Vandavar Barbarian raiders.
