## Role

The temple trains high priestesses and holds the Pagan festivals ([Beltane](../upgrades/festival-of-beltane.md), [Samhain](../upgrades/festival-of-samhain.md)) and the [Blessings of the Goddess](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-fertility.md). It turns your Manor into the Pagan Keep and unlocks heavy horsemen, mounted bowwomen and Vandavar Barbarian raiders.

## What it does

- **Trains** high priestesses; holds the festivals and the [Blessings of the Goddess](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-fertility.md) (300 seconds).
- **Sacrifices:** while one of its sacrifices runs, select peasants and right-click it ([Wickerman Sacrifice](../commands/wickerman-sacrifice.md)). Each peasant burned takes 10 seconds off the time.
- **Pagan Keep:** finishing it turns your Manor into the Pagan Keep.

## Defence

- **Strength:** 500.
- **What hurts it:** soldiers barely scratch it (0.1 a blow). Siege engines, sappers and fire do the work ([siege](../mechanics/siege.md#structural-damage)).
- **Fire:** each flaming arrow that hits it has a 2.5% chance to set it alight. Left alone, a fire grows until the building burns down, and it can leap to buildings within 25 m. Send peasants to [Extinguish Fire](../commands/extinguish.md).
- **Repair:** peasants [repair](../commands/repair.md) it, paying as they go ([strength and repair](../mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#strength-damage-and-repair)).
