## Role

The university is the Renaissance centre of research: gunpowder, rocketry, espionage, the bayonet and the advanced mills. It is expensive (1000 wood, 400 gold) and is hidden on the pure paths.

## Built by

<!-- gen:trained -->
- **Built by:** [Peasant](../units/peasant.md)
<!-- /gen:trained -->

## Needs

<!-- gen:needs -->
- building a [Temple of the Moon](black-mass.md) or building a [Cathedral](cathedral-stone.md) or building a [Observatory](observatory.md)
- Not on the pure paths: the button goes once you build a [Statue of the Archangel](michael-statue.md) or a [The Wickerman](wickerman.md).
<!-- /gen:needs -->
