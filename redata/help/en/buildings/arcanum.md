## Role

The arcanum researches [Explosives](../upgrades/grapeshot.md), the [DeathWyrm](../upgrades/infernal-engine.md) and the [Invoke Hecate](../upgrades/undead-legion.md). It needs a Temple of the Moon and an Observatory, and it turns your Castle into a Palace.

## What it does

- **Research:** Explosives, the DeathWyrm and Invoke Hecate (see the list below).
- **Dark portals:** with an Arcanum, druids and priestesses can open dark portals.
- **Palace:** finishing it turns your Castle into a Palace: Pagan if your Castle is Pagan, else Renaissance ([the five paths](../mechanics/alignment.md#the-five-paths)).

## Defence

- **Strength:** 2000. As strong as a Castle.
- **What hurts it:** soldiers barely scratch it (0.1 a blow). Siege engines, sappers and fire do the work ([siege](../mechanics/siege.md#structural-damage)).
- **Fire:** fireproof. Flaming arrows don't set it alight; a spy's arson, Greek fire and lightning still can.
- **Repair:** peasants [repair](../commands/repair.md) it, paying as they go ([strength and repair](../mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#strength-damage-and-repair)).
