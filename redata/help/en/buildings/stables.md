## Role

The stables train every horseman: mounted spearmen and javelineers from the start, then knights, heavy horsemen, mounted bowwomen and hand gunners depending on your path and research.

## What it does

- **Trains** every kind of horseman (see the list below).
- **Rally point:** set it on a [Training Dummy](training-dummy.md) and new riders start training at once.

## Defence

- **Strength:** 200. Weak: keep it inside your walls.
- **What hurts it:** soldiers barely scratch it (0.1 a blow). Siege engines, sappers and fire do the work ([siege](../mechanics/siege.md#structural-damage)).
- **Repair:** peasants [repair](../commands/repair.md) it, paying as they go ([strength and repair](../mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#strength-damage-and-repair)).
