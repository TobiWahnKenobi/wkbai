## Role

The Statue of the Archangel is the crown of the pure Imperial path. Building it unlocks men at arms, celestial knights and archers and war elephants, turns your pikemen and knights into their elite forms, and lets you make the [Sacrifice for Archangel](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-michael.md) that brings the archangel himself.

## What it does

- **When it is finished:** pikemen and knights become Imperial Pikemen and Imperial Knights (those you have and those you train), Imperial archers and war elephants open up, the Inquisitor and the acts [Inflict Wormwood](../commands/inflict-wormwood.md) and [Inflict Lightning](../commands/inflict-lightning.md) become possible, and each Cathedral holds up to 1200 piety.
- **The Archangel:** [Sacrifice for Archangel](../upgrades/sacrifice-for-michael.md) (600 seconds) brings the [Archangel](../units/michael.md).
- **Imperial Palace:** finishing it turns your Castle into the Imperial Palace.

## Defence

- **Strength:** 1000.
- **What hurts it:** soldiers barely scratch it (0.1 a blow). Siege engines, sappers and fire do the work ([siege](../mechanics/siege.md#structural-damage)).
- **Fire:** each flaming arrow that hits it has a 5% chance to set it alight. Left alone, a fire grows until the building burns down, and it can leap to buildings within 25 m. Send peasants to [Extinguish Fire](../commands/extinguish.md).
- **Repair:** peasants [repair](../commands/repair.md) it, paying as they go ([strength and repair](../mechanics/buildings-and-construction.md#strength-damage-and-repair)).
