# wkbai - the chronicle's words (interface/ChronicleNarrator). Written for wkbai, GPL-3 like the
# rest of the project. Edit freely: the game reads this file when it starts.
#
# Two kinds of entries.
#
# WORDS, one per line:   @NAME = one | another | a third
#   A word may have versions for a situation, tried first: @NAME.tag = ...
#   The tags of an event, in the order they are tried: its weather (clear, cloudy, rain,
#   heavyrain, storm, fog, snow, blizzard, heat, drought; heavyrain also tries rain, storm rain,
#   blizzard snow, drought heat), "cold" (below -2 degrees), "snowground" (snow lying), the time of
#   day (dawn, morning, noon, afternoon, dusk, night), the season (spring, summer, autumn, winter),
#   the war's stage (outset, early, mid, late), the size of a fight (skirmish, battle, great, huge),
#   and for a death its cause (cold, heat, drowned, ice, fire, lightning, slain, none).
#   An alternative may hold other words and facts in braces.
#
# SENTENCES, a block per slot:   [slot]   then one form per line until the next [slot]
#   A slot may have versions by tag too: [size.great] is used for a great battle.
#
# FACTS the game puts in (in braces, like words):
#   {DATE} the sentence's opening (from the date.* slots)   {PLACE} the place's name
#   {SIDES} the players in it ("Saint Jane and the Baron")  {SIDE} one player, {SIDE_S} with 's
#   {YEAR} {DAY} (an ordinal: "third")   {COUNT} fighters at its height (in words)
#   {MORE} {FEWER} the stronger and weaker side, {MORE_N} {FEWER_N} their numbers
#   {WINNER} {LOSER} {WINNER_S} {LOSER_S}   {LOST_HI} {LOST_LO} their dead (in words)
#   {TYPE_CLAUSE} ", most of them Spearmen" or nothing   {LOSSES} "Saint Jane three and the Baron ten"
#   {UNIT} a unit named (a link)   {KILLS} its kills in words   {KILLS_PHRASE} "three kills"
#   {HEROES} several heroes   {CAUSE_CLAUSE} ", taken by the cold" or nothing
#   {TYPES} the dead's type (in ALL_OF_THEM, MOST_OF_THEM)
#   {SETTLEMENT} "manor"   {A_SETTLEMENT} "a manor"   {OWNER_S} "the Baron's"   {RAZERS} who razed it
#   {VICTIM} the raided side   {KILLER} who slew a hero (in CAUSE.slain)
#
# "a" before a vowel becomes "an" by itself.
